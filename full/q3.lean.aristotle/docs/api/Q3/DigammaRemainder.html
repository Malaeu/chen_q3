    Stieltjes / Euler-Maclaurin remainder for digamma (N=1). Reference: Johansson (Maple Trans. 2023), equations (21) and (23), specialized to N=1 and differentiated to obtain the digamma identity.

    Proof skeleton and the arithmetic chain to the tail bound are recorded in PROWKA_RESPONSE_3.md.
