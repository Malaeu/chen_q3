\textbf{Background:} In the classical Weil criterion, the relevant target is a non-negative-definite quadratic form on a positive-definite cone generated by convolution squares, not positivity on every even nonnegative compactly supported bump. Our audit of the current Q3 contract therefore separates the broad ambient compact-support cone from the corrected positive-definite Weil cone.

\textbf{Main result:} We present a fully analytic proof of the normalization module \textup{(T0)}, the continuity module \textup{(A2)}, the uniform centered Toeplitz bridge \textup{(A3)}, and the RKHS prime cap \textup{(RKHS)}. We also record the shifted-atom density theorem \textup{(A1$'$)} on the auxiliary restriction cone $R_K=C^+_{\mathrm{even}}([-K,K])$. The target-cone audit shows that this broad restriction-level density does not by itself feed the honest Weil endgame. The corrected missing pair is instead \textup{(A1-pd)} plus packet-Rayleigh identification: density of centered Fej\'er$\times$heat / autocorrelation packets in the local positive-definite cone $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$, together with the exact quadratic-form bridge from those packets to the Toeplitz/RKHS engine.

\textbf{Conclusion:} Under the corrected local density theorem \textup{(A1-pd)} and packet-Rayleigh identification, the centered positivity engine \textup{(A3)}+\textup{(RKHS)} together with continuity and LF transfer yields $Q^\star(t;\Phi)\ge0$ on the global positive-definite Weil cone $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$, and Weil's criterion then implies the Riemann Hypothesis in our normalization.
