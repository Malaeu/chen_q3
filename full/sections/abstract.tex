\textbf{Background:} The Riemann Hypothesis (RH) is equivalent, by Weil, to the nonnegativity of the quadratic functional $Q^\star(t;\cdot)$ on an explicit cone of even, compactly supported test functions. Establishing $Q^\star(t;\Phi)\ge 0$ on the full Weil class requires a precise chain of analytic inputs: normalization, a restriction-level density theorem, continuity on admissible compact tests, a Toeplitz--symbol bridge, control of the prime contribution, and a compact-by-compact limit.

\textbf{Main result:} We present a fully analytic proof of the normalization module \textup{(T0)}, the continuity module \textup{(A2)}, the uniform Toeplitz bridge \textup{(A3)}, and the RKHS prime cap \textup{(RKHS)}. We also record the shifted-atom density theorem \textup{(A1$'$)} on the restriction cone $R_K=C^+_{\mathrm{even}}([-K,K])$ for each compact window $[-K,K]$. The Toeplitz bridge provides a positive symbol margin via Szeg\H{o}--B\"{o}ttcher theory and an explicit modulus of continuity, while a purely analytic RKHS contraction yields a uniform bound on the prime operator. The paper thereby isolates the exact closure issue: to deduce Weil positivity on the admissible compact class $W_K$, one needs a support upgrade from $R_K$ to $W_K$, a single admissible generator family $G_K\subseteq W_K$, and positivity on that same family.

\textbf{Conclusion:} Under the closure gates formulated in Section~\ref{sec:Main-closure} --- namely the support upgrade, the choice of a common admissible family $G_K$, positivity on that same family, and the LF lift --- the analytic modules imply $Q^\star(t;\Phi)\ge0$ for every $\Phi$ in the Weil cone $\mathcal W$, and Weil's positivity criterion then yields RH in our normalization.
