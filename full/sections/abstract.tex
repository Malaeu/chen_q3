\textbf{Background:} In the classical Weil criterion, the relevant target is a non-negative-definite quadratic form on a positive-definite cone generated by convolution squares, not positivity on every even nonnegative compactly supported bump. Our audit of the current Q3 contract therefore separates the broad ambient compact-support cone from the corrected positive-definite Weil cone.

\textbf{Main result:} We present a fully analytic proof of the normalization module \textup{(T0)}, the continuity module \textup{(A2)}, the uniform centered Toeplitz bridge \textup{(A3)}, and the RKHS prime cap \textup{(RKHS)}. We also record the shifted-atom density theorem \textup{(A1$'$)} on the auxiliary restriction cone $R_K=C^+_{\mathrm{even}}([-K,K])$. The target-cone audit shows that this broad restriction-level density does not by itself feed the honest Weil endgame. On the corrected positive-definite route, \textup{(A1-pd)} gives density for centered autocorrelation packets. The naive centered quadratic-form family $\Phi_{B,t}|p|^2$ remains mathematically natural, but it is too large to serve as the public closure family: combined with the full quadratic-form meaning of the Toeplitz estimate, it would overgenerate broad local positivity. The honest remaining knife-edge is therefore an exact \textup{(OP-pd)} theorem producing a \emph{smaller} operator-controlled packet family inside the corrected cone.

\textbf{Conclusion:} Under the corrected local density theorem \textup{(A1-pd)} and an exact operator-controlled packet theorem \textup{(OP-pd)} on the same corrected cone, the centered positivity engine \textup{(A3)}+\textup{(RKHS)} together with continuity and LF transfer yields $Q^\star(t;\Phi)\ge0$ on the global positive-definite Weil cone $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$, and Weil's criterion then implies the Riemann Hypothesis in our normalization.
