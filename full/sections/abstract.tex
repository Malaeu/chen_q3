\textbf{Background:} In the classical Weil criterion, the relevant target is a non-negative-definite quadratic form on a positive-definite cone generated by convolution squares, not positivity on every even nonnegative compactly supported bump. Our audit of the current Q3 contract therefore separates the broad ambient compact-support cone from the corrected positive-definite Weil cone.

\textbf{Main result:} We present a fully analytic proof of the normalization module \textup{(T0)}, the continuity module \textup{(A2)}, the uniform centered Toeplitz bridge \textup{(A3)}, and the RKHS prime cap \textup{(RKHS)}. We also record the shifted-atom density theorem \textup{(A1$'$)} on the auxiliary restriction cone $R_K=C^+_{\mathrm{even}}([-K,K])$. The target-cone audit shows that this broad restriction-level density does not by itself feed the honest Weil endgame. On the corrected positive-definite route, the density family and the operator-controlled family are now separated explicitly: \textup{(A1-pd)} gives density for centered autocorrelation packets, packet-Rayleigh identifies the centered Rayleigh family with the Toeplitz/RKHS quadratic form, and the honest remaining knife-edge is the same-family bridge between these two families.

\textbf{Conclusion:} Under the corrected local density theorem \textup{(A1-pd)}, packet-Rayleigh identification, and the same-family bridge closing the gap between density and positivity on the corrected cone, the centered positivity engine \textup{(A3)}+\textup{(RKHS)} together with continuity and LF transfer yields $Q^\star(t;\Phi)\ge0$ on the global positive-definite Weil cone $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$, and Weil's criterion then implies the Riemann Hypothesis in our normalization.
