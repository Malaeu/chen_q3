\begin{remark}[Dependency map]\label{rem:weil-dependency}
The sufficiency argument uses the following chain:
\[
  \text{(T0)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(G1)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(G2)}
  \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(G3)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(G4)}
  \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(G5)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(G6)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{RH}.
\]
Here \textup{(G1)} is the support upgrade from the restriction cone $R_K$ to admissible $W_K$,
\textup{(G2)} fixes one exact admissible family $G_K\subseteq W_K$,
\textup{(G3)} proves positivity on that same $G_K$,
\textup{(G4)} is the compact closure step on each $W_K$,
\textup{(G5)} is the LF lift to the Weil cone $\mathcal W$,
and \textup{(G6)} is Weil's criterion.
The analytic modules A1$'$, A2, A3, and RKHS feed these gates, but they do not by themselves close them.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Weil sufficiency pack]\label{thm:weil-sufficiency-pack}
Assume the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:Main-positivity}. Then $Q^\star(t;\Phi)\ge0$ for all $\Phi\in\mathcal W$, and hence the Riemann Hypothesis follows from Weil's positivity criterion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:rkhs-weil-isometry} the RKHS and Weil pictures are isometric on the working subspace.
Together with Lemmas~\ref{lem:rkhs-rayleigh-sampling-id} and \ref{a3:lem:prime-L2-trace} we transfer the mixed lower bound of Theorem~\ref{thm:A3}
to the quadratic functional $Q^\star(t;\cdot)$, while Corollary~\ref{a3:cor:A3_lock_formal} and the single-scale prime cap provide the centered positivity input that feeds the unresolved gate \textup{(G3)}. Theorem~\ref{thm:Main-positivity} then supplies positivity on the full Weil cone $\mathcal W$ once the closure gates are assumed.
Weil's criterion then yields the stated implication.
\end{proof}
