\begin{remark}[Dependency map]\label{rem:weil-dependency}
The sufficiency argument now uses the corrected chain:
\[
  \text{(T0-pd)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(A1-pd)}
  \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(packet-Rayleigh-pd)}
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  \text{centered (A3)+(RKHS)} + \text{(packet-Rayleigh-pd)}
  \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(PSD-pd)}
  \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(A2-pd)}
  \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(LF-pd)}
  \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{(G6)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{RH}.
\]
Here \textup{(A1-pd)} is the centered packet density theorem on the corrected local
cone $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$, while \textup{(packet-Rayleigh-pd)} is the exact
Toeplitz quadratic-form identity on autocorrelation packets
$\Psi_c * \widetilde{\Psi_c}$. The naive family $\Phi_{B,t}|p|^2$ remains available as
background quadratic-form evidence, but it is too large to serve as the public
closure family. The old uniform packet-symbol route \textup{(A3-pd)} is also too
strong on a dense packet dictionary. The new hard theorem \textup{(PSD-pd)} is
positive semidefiniteness of the packet kernel
$K_Q(g_i,g_j)=Q^\star(t;g_i * \widetilde{g_j})$ on that same exact dense pre-packet
space. The clean Herglotz/Bochner language remains useful as a diagnostic
equivalence for positive-definite packet coefficients, but the primary
constructive route is now direct full-kernel positivity: exact packet
sesquilinear identity, then the prime-block obstruction on packet space, then a
full-symbol domination criterion $A_{g,\Delta}\ge P_{g,\Delta}$ or a new operator
package for the full kernel. Centered \textup{(A3)+(RKHS)} supplies the model
estimates that must be upgraded to this packet-kernel positivity,
\textup{(A2-pd)} is continuity on the corrected local cone,
\textup{(LF-pd)} is the inductive-limit lift to the global positive-definite cone
$\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$, and \textup{(G6)} is Weil's criterion.
The shifted theorem A1$'$ on $R_K$ remains available as auxiliary density on a
broader cone, but it is no longer the mainline RH density theorem.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Weil sufficiency pack]\label{thm:weil-sufficiency-pack}
Assume the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:Main-positivity}. Then
$Q^\star(t;\Phi)\ge0$ for all $\Phi\in\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$, and hence the
Riemann Hypothesis follows from Weil's positivity criterion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:rkhs-weil-isometry} the RKHS and Weil pictures are isometric on
the working subspace. Together with Lemmas~\ref{lem:rkhs-rayleigh-sampling-id} and
\ref{a3:lem:prime-L2-trace} and the exact operator-family theorem from
Section~\ref{sec:Main-closure}, we transfer the mixed lower bound of
Theorem~\ref{thm:A3} to the quadratic functional $Q^\star(t;\cdot)$ on the exact
packet family through Theorem~\ref{thm:packet-rayleigh-pd}, while the single-scale
prime cap provides the centered positivity input that must be upgraded to
Theorem~\ref{thm:PSD-pd}. Theorem~\ref{thm:Main-positivity} then supplies positivity on
the full corrected Weil cone $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$ once the dense corrected route
and PSD packet-kernel theorem are assumed. Weil's criterion then yields the
stated implication.
\end{proof}
