\noindent The density module A1$'$ is currently formulated on the restriction cone $R_K$ using the shifted family $\Phi_{B,t,\tau}$, while A2 and the LF route live on the admissible support cone $W_K$. The unresolved closure gates are therefore: support upgrade from $R_K$ to $W_K$, identification of one exact admissible family $G_K\subseteq W_K$, and positivity on that same $G_K$.
