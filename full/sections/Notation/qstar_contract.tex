\noindent The target-cone audit of Section~\ref{sec:scope} demotes the broad compact-support cone
to an ambient auxiliary space and promotes the positive-definite /
convolution-square cone $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$ as the public local target for the
Weil criterion. Accordingly:
\begin{itemize}
  \item A1$'$ remains a theorem on the broad restriction cone $R_K$;
  \item A2 supplies continuity on the ambient compact-support side and therefore on
  $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$ in particular;
  \item the density theorem A1-pd concerns the family
  $\mathcal G_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$ inside $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$;
  \item the naive packet-Rayleigh identity on
  $\mathcal G_{K,\mathrm{Ray}}^{\mathrm{pd}}$ is background-only, because that family
  is too large to serve as the public closure family;
  \item the exact packet-Rayleigh theorem lives on autocorrelation packets
  $\Psi_c * \widetilde{\Psi_c}$ with finite Toeplitz block $T_J(\kappa)$ and
  finite symbol $S_J$ on each admissible dictionary;
  \item the compact scalar package uses the compact-localized functional
  $\mathcal Q_K$ and the spectral weight $W_K(u)$; on tests supported in
  $[-K,K]$ one has $\mathcal Q_K=\mathcal Q$, and on even such tests
  $\mathcal Q_K=Q^\star(t;\cdot)$;
  \item the old route \textup{A3-pd} --- one uniform packet-symbol floor on the full dense
  packet dictionary --- is too strong and therefore rejected as the public theorem shape;
  \item the sparse Gershgorin route is kept only as a local finite-block lemma
  and explicitly not as the dense main theorem;
  \item the honest remaining knife-edge is \textup{PSD-pd}: prove that the packet kernel
  $K_Q(g_i,g_j)=\mathcal Q(g_i * \widetilde{g_j})$ is positive semidefinite on a
  dense translation-compatible packet subspace;
  \item the scalar compact package is retained only as a correct
  compact-truncation reduction and no longer serves as the live public route
  once $\Xi_K\neq\varnothing$;
  \item the primary live constructive target is now the Suzuki/Yoshida
  generalized form-pair bridge \textup{H1} $\to$ \textup{H2} $\to$ \textup{H3} $\to$ \textup{H4};
  \item the finite admissible dictionary criterion $S_J(\theta)\ge0$ on each
  compact packet block is retained as fallback discretization / verification,
  together with Poisson-regularized finite symbols $S_{J,r}$ and an explicit error budget;
  \item the live quantitative frontier is the concrete packet bounding package:
  diagonal Archimedean core/tail bounds for $\alpha_0$, off-diagonal
  localization/modulus bounds for $\alpha_m$, local symmetric prime-mass bounds
  for $\beta_m$, and the explicit sufficient inequalities \textup{(C1)} and
  \textup{(C1$'$)} on each finite admissible dictionary;
  \item the canonical packet $g_{\delta,t_0,0}$ now serves as the first pilot
  model: its support radii, exact norms, and lower bound $H_r\ge M_g(r/2)^2$
  make the finite criterion numerically testable on small sparse dictionaries;
  \item the measure-level/full-symbol language $\mu_A-\mu_P\ge 0$ remains useful
  only as a secondary Herglotz-style equivalence, not as the first constructive
  target for compact positivity;
  \item the centered A3/RKHS positivity mechanism is kept as the positivity engine
  for that corrected cone.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{WRONG public target:} broad positivity on every even nonnegative compactly supported test.\\
\textbf{RIGHT public target:} positivity on the corrected positive-definite Weil cone
$\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$, generated locally by convolution squares / centered packet cones.
\end{tcolorbox}
