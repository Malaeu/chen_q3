\section{Positioning and Scope}\label{sec:scope}

This work introduces a quantitative, modular operator framework for the Weil criterion. The present draft closes the analytic modules \textup{(T0)}, \textup{(A2)}, \textup{(A3)}, and \textup{(RKHS)}, and it records the shifted density theorem \textup{(A1$'$)} on a broader restriction cone. The target-cone audit performed in the current reset shows that the public RH route cannot continue on the broad cone of all even nonnegative compactly supported tests. The live scope is therefore the corrected positive-definite Weil cone.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{What this is:} A unified analytic program with explicit constants (symbol modulus, RKHS cap, node spacing, tail control) whose current output is a typed conditional route to positivity on the corrected positive-definite Weil cone.
  \item \textbf{What this is not:} This draft does not yet claim that the corrected closure chain is fully closed. The unresolved knife-edge is now packet-kernel positivity on finite admissible packet dictionaries inside the corrected cone. The naive centered family $\Phi_{B,t}|p|^2$ is too large to serve as the public closure family.
  \item \textbf{Modularity:} Local improvements in the Archimedean symbol, the RKHS cap, or the packet density theorem feed only the corresponding module and do not change the rest of the chain.
  \item \textbf{Test class:} For each $K>0$ we distinguish
  \[
    R_K := C^+_{\mathrm{even}}([-K,K]),
    \qquad
    B_K := \bigl\{\Phi\in C_c(\mathbb R): \operatorname{supp}\Phi\subset[-K,K],\ \Phi\ \text{even}\bigr\},
  \]
  and, writing
  \[
    \widetilde{\psi}(x):=\overline{\psi(-x)},
    \qquad
    \mathcal W_{K,0}^{\mathrm{pd}}
    :=\bigl\{\psi*\widetilde{\psi}:\ \psi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R),\ \operatorname{supp}\psi\subset[-K/2,K/2]\bigr\},
  \]
  the corrected local Weil-positive cone
  \[
    \mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}
    := \overline{\operatorname{cone}\!\bigl(\mathcal W_{K,0}^{\mathrm{pd}}\bigr)}^{\|\cdot\|_\infty}
    \subset B_K.
  \]
  The public global target is
  \[
    \mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}} := \varinjlim_{K\to\infty}\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}.
  \]
  The theorem A1$'$ is now auxiliary density on the broad restriction cone $R_K$, whereas the centered density family
  \[
    \mathcal G_K^{\mathrm{pd}}
    := \operatorname{cone}\!\Bigl\{
      \Psi*\widetilde{\Psi}:
      \Psi \text{ finite Fej\'er}\times\text{heat pre-packet with }
      \operatorname{supp}\Psi\subset[-K/2,K/2]
    \Bigr\}
  \]
  feeds the corrected mainline density theorem A1-pd. On the same exact packet side,
  the packet-Rayleigh theorem identifies
  \[
    \mathcal Q(\Psi_c * \widetilde{\Psi_c})
    = \langle T_M[S_{g,\Delta}]c,c\rangle
  \]
  for the Toeplitz symbol $S_{g,\Delta}$ built from packet coefficients
  $\kappa_m=\mathcal Q(h(\cdot-m\Delta))$, while the naive centered Rayleigh family
  \[
    \mathcal G_{K,\mathrm{Ray}}^{\mathrm{pd}}
    := \operatorname{cone}\!\bigl\{\Phi_{B,t,p}=\Phi_{B,t}|p|^2\bigr\}
  \]
  is only a background quadratic-form candidate. The live frontier is no longer
  a uniform packet-symbol floor on the dense packet dictionary. The honest
  remaining theorem is `PSD-pd`: positive semidefiniteness of the packet kernel
  \[
    K_Q(g_i,g_j):=\mathcal Q(g_i * \widetilde{g_j})
  \]
  on a dense translation-compatible packet subspace, with finite-symbol domination
  $S_J=A_J-P_J\ge0$ on each admissible dictionary as the immediate constructive
  target. The live quantitative frontier is the coefficient package for
  $\alpha_m$ and $\beta_m$: Archimedean support/localization and core-tail
  bounds, local symmetric prime-mass bounds, and the explicit sufficient
  inequalities \textup{(C1)} and \textup{(C1$'$)}. Poisson-regularized finite
  symbols remain a verification device, and Gershgorin is retained only as a
  sparse finite-block criterion. The canonical centered half-atom
  $g_{\delta,t_0,0}$ is now the first pilot packet: its exact norms, the lower
  bound $H_r\ge M_g(r/2)^2$, and the compact test case $K=0.2$, $J=\{0,1\}$,
  $\Delta=0.15$ show that the finite criterion is genuinely nonvacuous before
  any dense-closure attempt.
  \item \textbf{Verification path:} Sections~\ref{sec:T0}--\ref{sec:rkhs-prime-cap} supply the fully written analytic modules. Section~\ref{sec:Main-closure} now packages the corrected positive-definite closure route. Appendix~\ref{app:legacy-branch} keeps legacy broad-cone branches as read-only provenance only.
  \item \textbf{Computation:} Symbol scans, ATP logs, and certificate tables are reproducibility aids only; they do not enter the logical core of the active proof chain.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\emph{Bridge summary.} We split $Q^\star(t;\Phi_{B,t})$ via the Rayleigh identification into a Toeplitz block and a Rayleigh prime block, i.e., $T_M[P_A](t,B,M)$ versus $T_P^{\mathrm{Ray}}(t,M)$. The symbol barrier yields $\lambda_{\min}(T_M[P_A])\ge c_* - C\,\omega_{P_A}(1/(2M))$, where $c_* = \tfrac{11}{10}$ is the \textbf{uniform Archimedean floor} from Lemma~\ref{lem:uniform-arch-floor} (for $t=t_{\mathrm{crit}}=\tfrac{3}{20}=0.15$, $B_{\min}=3$), valid on the full circle $\TT$ and independent of $K$. The prime norm is bounded in the Arch-induced RKHS by the single-scale cap
\[
  \|T_P^{\mathrm{RKHS}}(t_{\mathrm{crit}})\|\le \rho(t_{\mathrm{crit}})\le c_*/4,
\]
at the bridge scale $t_{\mathrm{crit}}=3/20$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:uniform-prime-cap}). Thus
\[
  \lambda_{\min}(T_M[P_A]-T_P^{\mathrm{Ray}}(t,M))\ \ge\ \min P_A\ -\ C\,\omega_{P_A}(1/(2M))\ -\ \rho(t),
\]
closing the centered A3/RKHS analytic bridge. What remains is not another shifted positivity theorem but the correct density route from centered packets to the positive-definite Weil cone.
What remains is not another shifted positivity theorem and not a same-family bridge through $\Phi_{B,t}|p|^2$. The live missing theorem is an exact smaller operator-controlled packet family that stays inside the corrected positive-definite cone.

\section{Gate Contract}\label{sec:global-hyp}

For reference we collect the live proof contract used after the target-cone audit.

\begin{description}
  \item[(T0-pd)] Guinand--Weil normalization of $Q^\star(t;\cdot)$ together with the corrected positive-definite target cone.
  \item[(A1-pd)] Density of the centered autocorrelation family $\mathcal G_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$ in $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$.
  \item[(packet-Rayleigh-naive)] Background quadratic-form identity on the overlarge family $\mathcal G_{K,\mathrm{Ray}}^{\mathrm{pd}}=\operatorname{cone}\{\Phi_{B,t}|p|^2\}$.
  \item[(packet-Rayleigh-pd)] Exact Toeplitz quadratic-form identity on autocorrelation packets $\Psi_c * \widetilde{\Psi_c}$ with packet symbol $S_{g,\Delta}$.
  \item[(A3-pd)] Rejected-too-strong route: one uniform packet-symbol floor on the full dense packet dictionary.
  \item[(PSD-pd)] Positive semidefiniteness of the packet kernel $K_Q(g_i,g_j)=\mathcal Q(g_i * \widetilde{g_j})$ on a dense translation-compatible packet subspace.
  \item[(A3)+(RKHS)] Centered positivity mechanism at the critical scale.
  \item[(A2-pd)] Continuity of $Q^\star(t;\cdot)$ on the ambient compact-support class, hence on $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$ in particular.
  \item[(LF-pd)] Inductive-limit lift from all $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$ to $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$.
  \item[(G6)] Weil linkage: positivity on $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$ implies RH.
\end{description}

The present manuscript establishes the analytic inputs behind this chain as follows:

\begin{itemize}
  \item `T0` in Section~\ref{sec:T0};
  \item `A1$'$` as an auxiliary density theorem on $R_K$ in Section~\ref{sec:A1};
  \item `A2` as continuity on admissible compact tests in Section~\ref{sec:A2};
  \item `A3` and `RKHS` as the centered positivity mechanism in Sections~\ref{sec:A3} and \ref{sec:RKHS}.
\end{itemize}

What remains unresolved in the present draft is not the corrected cone itself, nor the theorem-level packet-Rayleigh identity. Pushing the naive Rayleigh route further shows that the family $\Phi_{B,t}|p|^2$ is too large to serve as the closure family, and pushing the old `A3-pd` route further shows that one uniform packet-symbol floor cannot hold on a dense packet dictionary. The actual knife-edge is therefore `PSD-pd`: prove positive semidefiniteness of the packet kernel $K_Q(g_i,g_j)=\mathcal Q(g_i * \widetilde{g_j})$ on a dense translation-compatible packet subspace. The immediate constructive target is now finite-symbol domination on each admissible dictionary,
\[
  S_J(\theta)=A_J(\theta)-P_J(\theta)\ge0,
\]
verified by Poisson-regularized finite symbols and an explicit error budget. The next explicit packet-level goal is to bound $\alpha_m$ and $\beta_m$ strongly enough to trigger \textup{(C1)} or \textup{(C1$'$)} on admissible finite dictionaries. Measure-level/full-symbol language remains secondary Herglotz notation, and the Gershgorin route survives only as a sparse finite-block lemma. Once that theorem package is supplied, the rest of the route is the routine closure / LF / Weil tail.
The new pilot computation for $g_{\delta,t_0,0}$ confirms both sides of the
story: sparse finite-dictionary positivity can really close, but the same
diagonal-dominance mechanism still does not promise dense closure.

\section{Notation and Conventions}\label{sec:notation}

On the frequency axis we write $\xi=\eta/(2\pi)$. In the Toeplitz bridge we work on the unit circle
\(\TT=\RR/\ZZ\) with fundamental domain $[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12]$. The Archimedean density is
\[
  a(\xi)=\log\pi-\Re\psi\Big(\tfrac14+i\pi\xi\Big),\qquad a_*(\xi)=2\pi\,a(\xi),
\]
and prime nodes are at $\xi_n=\tfrac{\log n}{2\pi}$ with symmetric placement $\pm\xi_n$. We distinguish two weight conventions:
\[
\begin{aligned}
  w_Q(n) &= \frac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt{n}}
  &&\text{(the one-sided weight inside $Q^\ast(t;\Phi)$)}, \\
  w_{\mathrm{RKHS}}(n) &= \frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt{n}}
  &&\text{(the operator weight in the RKHS analysis)}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Evenization lets us pass freely between them: doubling $w_{\mathrm{RKHS}}$ on $\xi_n>0$ gives $w_Q$, while placing both $\pm\xi_n$ leaves $w_{\mathrm{RKHS}}$ unchanged. All RKHS and operator bounds below use $w_{\mathrm{RKHS}}$; we abbreviate $w_{\max}:=\sup_n w_{\mathrm{RKHS}}(n)\le 2/e$. Throughout we use
\[
  Q^\star(t;\Phi)=\int_{\mathbb{R}} a_*(\xi)\,\Phi(\xi)\,d\xi\;-\;\sum_{n\ge2} w_Q(n)\,\Phi(\xi_n),
\]
with the bridge scale fixed at $t=t_{\mathrm{crit}}$; Section~\ref{sec:T0} records the exact crosswalk to the Guinand--Weil form. For the public RH route the target is not the broad compact-support cone, but the positive-definite cone $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$ generated locally by convolution squares.

\subsection{Contemporary Context and Inspiration}

This work was inspired by several recent developments in analytic number theory, computational complexity, and mathematical logic:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Analytic criteria.} Li's positivity sequence~\cite{Li1997} and the Jensen polynomial programme of Griffin--Ono--Rolen--Zagier~\cite{GriffinOnoRolenZagier2019} give logically equivalent restatements of RH; both inspire our insistence on keeping every cone generator and Lipschitz bound explicit.

  \item \textbf{Zero-density breakthroughs.} The new Dirichlet-polynomial bounds of Guth and Maynard~\cite{GuthMaynard2024} illustrate how much can be gained by encoding the zeta problem as a spectral estimate, a viewpoint we adopt through the Toeplitz bridge.

  \item \textbf{Near-miss invariants.} Rodgers and Tao's work on the de Bruijn--Newman constant~\cite{rodgers2020debruijn} shows that RH may be ``barely true'', motivating a watchdog table that certifies every slack we introduce along the chain.

  \item \textbf{Geometric and noncommutative ideas.} Fesenko's two-dimensional adelic programme~\cite{Fesenko2008} and the Connes--Marcolli noncommutative approach~\cite{ConnesMarcolli2008} highlight how positivity hinges on careful operator factorizations, reinforcing our choice to stay within verifiable Toeplitz/RKHS settings.

  \item \textbf{Physical operator heuristics.} PT-symmetric constructions such as Bender--Brody--M\"uller~\cite{BenderBrodyMuller2017} keep the Hilbert--P\'olya dream alive; our framework aims to supply the missing rigorous operator inequalities.

  \item \textbf{Geometric flows and smoothing.} Perelman's Ricci-flow programme~\cite{Perelman2002,Perelman2003} shows how parabolic averaging can enforce global structure; we mirror that philosophy by pairing Fej\'er kernels with heat-flow smoothing in the Toeplitz bridge.

  \item \textbf{Programmatic limit architectures.} Deng--Hani--Ma~\cite{DengHaniMa2025Hilbert6} present a two-step limiting program (Newtonian dynamics $\to$ Boltzmann $\to$ fluid equations) that highlights how long-time control stabilizes successive limits; our compact-by-compact transfer plays an analogous ``bridge'' role for the Weil criterion.

  \item \textbf{Trace-formula-to-gap pipelines.} Anantharaman--Monk~\cite{AnantharamanMonk2025FR} use length-spectrum data and trace-formula technology to obtain asymptotic spectral gaps for random hyperbolic surfaces, a context that reinforces why spectral/trace language is a natural narrative for Weil-criterion positivity (here ``Weil--Petersson'' is unrelated to the Weil criterion).

  \item \textbf{Massive computations.} Platt and Trudgian's verification of RH up to $3\cdot10^{12}$~\cite{PlattTrudgian2021}, together with surveys like Conrey's~\cite{Conrey2003}, emphasise the need for transparent, audit-friendly proofs rather than ever-larger numerics.

  \item \textbf{Cautionary analyses.} Cairo's audit of proposed counterexamples~\cite{cairo2025counterexample} underlines how fragile heuristic arguments can be; we therefore keep every analytic assumption explicit and machine-checkable.
\end{itemize}

\noindent While these works influenced our methodology, our approach is fundamentally distinct: we construct a self-contained, verifiable chain from centered Toeplitz/RKHS positivity to Weil positivity on the corrected positive-definite cone, with all critical steps amenable to formal verification.
