\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection*{Background and motivation}
We study the Guinand--Weil quadratic form through the analytic module stack \textup{(T0)}+\textup{(A2)}+\textup{(A3)}+\textup{(RKHS)}, together with the auxiliary shifted density theorem \textup{(A1$'$)} on a broad restriction cone. The entire argument is analytic: every bound is established on paper from explicit inequalities, the parameters are given in closed form, and the uniform bridge avoids K-dependent schedules. No numerical tables or automated certificates enter the logical core. The main conceptual correction of the present draft is that the RH endgame must run through the positive-definite / convolution-square Weil cone, not through the broad cone of all even nonnegative compactly supported tests.

\subsection*{Main result}
\begin{theorem}[Main result, informal]\label{thm:intro-main-informal}
Let $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$ be the corrected positive-definite Weil cone from Section~\ref{sec:scope}. Assume that the local centered packet density theorem \textup{(A1-pd)}, the exact packet-Rayleigh theorem on autocorrelation packets, the PSD packet-kernel theorem \textup{(PSD-pd)} on the same corrected cone, and the corresponding LF closure on $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$ have been established in addition to the analytic modules written in the present draft. Then
\[
  Q^\star(t;\Phi)\ \ge\ 0\qquad\text{for all }\Phi\in\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}},
\]
and via Theorem~\ref{thm:Weil-criterion} this positivity is equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis.
\end{theorem}

\subsection*{Program diagram}
Our proof is organized as a controlled-limit program: a small number of analytic bridges are composed, and each bridge is driven by explicit parameter schedules along a compact exhaustion. The active logical chain is:

\begin{center}
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{c}
    `T0-pd` normalization and corrected target cone \\
    $\Downarrow$ \\
    `A1-pd` centered packet density in $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$ \\
    $\Downarrow$ \\
    exact packet-Rayleigh on autocorrelation packets $\Psi_c * \widetilde{\Psi_c}$ \\
    $\Downarrow$ \\
    `PSD-pd` packet-kernel positivity on that same family $\Longrightarrow$ `A2` local closure on $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$ \\
    $\Downarrow$ \\
    `LF-pd` lift to $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$ $\Longrightarrow$ `G6` Weil criterion $\Longrightarrow$ RH
  \end{tabular}
\end{center}

The present draft fully develops the analytic inputs that feed this chain, but it still leaves open the PSD packet-kernel theorem `PSD-pd`. The naive centered family $\Phi_{B,t}|p|^2$ is now background-only, because as a closure family it is too large, and the old `A3-pd` uniform-gap route on the dense packet family is also too strong.

\subsection*{Archimedean bridge}
\emph{(A3) Archimedean Toeplitz barrier.} The Toeplitz component $T_M[P_A]$ in the Rayleigh decomposition of $Q^\star(t;\Phi_{B,t})$ is bounded from below by the explicit uniform Archimedean floor $c_*$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:uniform-arch-floor}), up to the controllable Lipschitz loss $C\,\omega_{P_A}(1/(2M))$. Szeg\H{o}--B\"ottcher asymptotics together with an explicit modulus of continuity for $P_A$ yield
\[
  \lambda_{\min}\!\big(T_M[P_A]\big)\ \ge\ c_*\ -\ C\,\omega_{P_A}\!\Big(\frac{1}{2M}\Big),
\]
as developed in Section~\ref{sec:A3}.

\subsection*{Prime contraction}
\emph{(RKHS) Uniform prime contraction without tables.} The prime contribution is encoded by the RKHS operator $T_P^{\mathrm{RKHS}}(t)$ supported on the nodes $\xi_n=\frac{\log n}{2\pi}$; in the Weil functional we use the one-sided weights $w_Q(n)=2\Lambda(n)/\sqrt{n}$, while in the RKHS analysis we keep the undoubled operator weights $w_{\mathrm{RKHS}}(n)=\Lambda(n)/\sqrt{n}$. Section~\ref{sec:rkhs-prime-cap} develops a tables-free cap from the closed form $\rho(t)$. For the main A3 bridge we use the single-scale evaluation at $t=t_{\mathrm{crit}}=3/20$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:uniform-prime-cap}).

\subsection*{Target-cone correction}
The target-cone audit of the current reset has one hard outcome: the broad cone
\[
  B_K=\{\Phi\in C_c(\mathbb R): \operatorname{supp}\Phi\subset[-K,K],\ \Phi\ \text{even}\}
\]
is too wide to serve as the public RH target. The classical Weil criterion is quadratic / positive-definite in nature, so the corrected local object is
\[
  \widetilde{\psi}(x):=\overline{\psi(-x)},
  \qquad
  \mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}
  := \overline{\operatorname{cone}\{\psi * \widetilde{\psi}:\ \psi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R),\ \operatorname{supp}\psi\subset[-K/2,K/2]\}}^{\|\cdot\|_\infty},
\]
and the global target is $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}=\varinjlim_{K>0}\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$.

\subsection*{Compact extension}
\emph{(A1$'$ vs. A1-pd).} The shifted density theorem A1$'$ is naturally a theorem on the broad restriction cone
\[
  R_K = C^+_{\mathrm{even}}([-K,K]).
\]
It is still useful as auxiliary approximation technology, but it no longer feeds the RH mainline directly. The new theorem package is: A1-pd on the dense autocorrelation side, exact packet-Rayleigh on that same packet side, and then `PSD-pd` as the single live knife-edge. Once packet-kernel positivity is closed, continuity from A2 gives the local closure step, and the LF tail becomes routine.

\subsection*{Outline of the proof}

Combining the Toeplitz barrier and the uniform RKHS cap yields, on the centered bridge family,
\[
  \lambda_{\min}\!\big(T_M[P_A]-T_P^{\mathrm{Ray}}(t,M)\big)
  \ \ge\ c_*\ -\ C\,\omega_{P_A}\!\Big(\frac{1}{2M}\Big)\ -\ \rho(t).
\]
Selecting $M\ge M_0^{\mathrm{unif}}$ gives $C\,\omega_{P_A}(1/(2M))\le c_*/2$, and
choosing $t=t_{\mathrm{crit}}=3/20$ yields $\rho(t_{\mathrm{crit}})\le c_*/4$, so
\[
  \lambda_{\min}\!\big(T_M[P_A]-T_P^{\mathrm{Ray}}(t,M)\big)\ \ge\ \frac{c_*}{4}\ >\ 0.
\]
Hence the analytic core already produces a centered positivity mechanism. The remaining work is to identify the correct local cone, prove `A1-pd`, freeze exact packet-Rayleigh on the same autocorrelation packet family, and then prove the PSD packet-kernel theorem `PSD-pd`; only after that do the local closure, LF lift, and Weil criterion finish RH.

\subsection*{What is new}

Two features distinguish the present work.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{A consistent centered positivity engine.} The Archimedean symbol is defined once via periodization of the Fej\'er$\times$heat window, and its Lipschitz modulus is proved directly for that same object, eliminating mixed definitions.
  \item \textbf{A corrected RH target.} The draft now separates the broad ambient compact-support cone from the corrected positive-definite Weil cone, and it isolates the exact knife-edge: the dense autocorrelation family survives, exact packet-Rayleigh survives on that same packet side, while the naive quadratic-form family $\Phi_{B,t}|p|^2$ is demoted to background-only because it is too large and the old `A3-pd` uniform-gap route is too strong. The live blocker is now the PSD packet-kernel theorem `PSD-pd`.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Notation}

We write $\Lambda$ for the von Mangoldt function, $\xi_n=\frac{\log n}{2\pi}$ for the sampling nodes, $w_Q(n)=2\Lambda(n)/\sqrt{n}$ for the weights inside the Weil functional, and $w_{\mathrm{RKHS}}(n)=\Lambda(n)/\sqrt{n}$ (with $w_{\max}=\sup_n w_{\mathrm{RKHS}}(n)\le 2/e$) for the operator analysis. The heat kernel is $k_t(x,y)=\exp\!\bigl(-\frac{(x-y)^2}{4t}\bigr)$. For each compact window $[-K,K]$ we distinguish the broad restriction cone $R_K=C^+_{\mathrm{even}}([-K,K])$, the broad ambient support class $B_K$ of even compactly supported tests supported in $[-K,K]$, and the corrected positive-definite cone $\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$. The public target is $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}=\bigcup_{K>0}\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$. Complete conventions appear in Section~\ref{sec:notation}.
