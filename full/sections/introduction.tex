\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection*{Background and motivation}
We study the Guinand--Weil quadratic form through the analytic module stack \textup{(T0)}+\textup{(A2)}+\textup{(A3)}+\textup{(RKHS)}, together with the auxiliary shifted density theorem \textup{(A1$'$)} on a broad restriction cone. The entire argument is analytic: every bound is established on paper from explicit inequalities, the parameters are given in closed form, and the uniform bridge avoids K-dependent schedules. No numerical tables or automated certificates enter the logical core. The main conceptual correction of the present draft is that the RH endgame must run through the positive-definite / convolution-square Weil cone, not through the broad cone of all even nonnegative compactly supported tests.

\subsection*{Main result}
\begin{theorem}[Main result, informal]\label{thm:intro-main-informal}
Let $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$ be the corrected positive-definite Weil cone from Section~\ref{sec:scope}. Assume that the local centered packet density theorem \textup{(A1-pd)}, the exact packet-Rayleigh theorem on autocorrelation packets, the PSD packet-kernel theorem \textup{(PSD-pd)} on the same corrected cone, and the corresponding LF closure on $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}$ have been established in addition to the analytic modules written in the present draft. Then
\[
  Q^\star(t;\Phi)\ \ge\ 0\qquad\text{for all }\Phi\in\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}},
\]
and via Theorem~\ref{thm:Weil-criterion} this positivity is equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis.
\end{theorem}

\noindent In the current reset, the fastest live new-theorem route is the
Suzuki/Yoshida generalized form-pair bridge
\[
  \textup{H1} \Longrightarrow \textup{H2} \Longrightarrow \textup{H3} \Longrightarrow \textup{H4}.
\]
The fallback Weil-side route remains
\[
  \textup{A1-pd} + \textup{packet-Rayleigh-pd} + \textup{PSD-pd}.
\]
The compact scalar package through $W_K(u)$ is retained only as a correct
diagnostic reduction, not as the live public route.

\subsection*{Program diagram}
Our proof is organized as a controlled-limit program: a small number of analytic bridges are composed, and each bridge is driven by explicit parameter schedules along a compact exhaustion. The active logical chain is:

\begin{center}
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{c}
    \textup{T0-pd} normalization and corrected target cone \\
    $\Downarrow$ \\
    Suzuki/Yoshida generalized form-pair bridge \textup{H1} $\to$ \textup{H2} $\to$ \textup{H3} $\to$ \textup{H4} \\
    $\Downarrow$ \\
    no zero eigenvalue for $G_g[a]$ for every $a>0$ \\
    $\Downarrow$ \\
    RH by Suzuki Theorem 1.4
  \end{tabular}
\end{center}

The present draft fully develops the analytic inputs that feed this chain, but
the live blocker is no longer the scalar compact inequality
\[
  W_K(u)\ge0\qquad\text{for every compact }K>0\text{ and every }u\in\mathbb R.
\]
On nontrivial compacts this pointwise target is obstructed: $a_K^*\in L^1$
forces $\widehat{a_K^*}(u)\to0$, while the finite positive cosine prime sum
over $\Xi_K$ returns arbitrarily close to its full mass infinitely often. So the
compact spectral package `S1/S2/S3/S4` is kept only as a correct diagnostic
reduction. The naive centered family $\Phi_{B,t}|p|^2$ is background-only,
because as a closure family it is too large, and the old \textup{A3-pd} uniform-gap
route on the dense packet family is also too strong. The clean
`Herglotz/Bochner' reformulation survives as a diagnostic equivalence, but the
literal packet-level ``prime block PSD, then Arch dominates'' route is rejected on
packet space. The strict finite-dictionary package is retained as fallback
discretization: exact packet sesquilinear identity through the symmetrically
extended compact functional (`P1`), Toeplitz reduction on each finite
admissible dictionary \textup{(P2)}, prime-block obstruction \textup{(P4)}, full sequence split
\textup{(P5)}, Toeplitz--Herglotz criterion \textup{(P6)}, and then the finite-symbol package
\textup{(P7.3)}--\textup{(P7.6)}: exact finite symbol identity, finite-symbol domination $S_J\ge0$,
and Poisson-regularized verification with explicit error budget. What is now
concretely missing is \textup{H1}: build $S_{a,M}$ and $J_a$ so that the finite
Hermitian Q3 energy is pulled back as a generalized form pair for Suzuki's
operator criterion. The first candidate is already clear: use a nonorthogonal
packet synthesis on $(-a,a)$ extracted from the same centered
Fej\'er$\times$heat / RKHS geometry as the Q3 finite block, and let $J_a$ be
the corresponding Gram-pullback metric on the synthesized packet span.
The preferred first-pass refinement is filtered Volterra: use
$J_a=(I_0^{(a)})^*I_0^{(a)}$ and characterize $S_{a,M}$ by
$I_0^{(a)}S_{a,M}=U_aM_{1+z}|_{P_M}$. Then the pullback metric is the explicit
Toeplitz block
\[
  B_M=T_M[|1+z|^2]=\Delta_+^*\Delta_+,
  \qquad
  \Delta_+=I+L,
\]
and the correct filtered finite section is
\[
  \widetilde Q_M:=\Delta_+^*Q_{M+1}\Delta_+.
\]
Hence any bulk bound $Q_{M+1}\ge c(a)I$ transfers directly to
$\widetilde Q_M\ge c(a)B_M$, with no loss of the old factor $1/4$. Finite-prime
semilocal data remain useful only as a secondary basis/Gram engine for
\textup{H1}, not as a new RH endgame in their own right.
The first canonical test case is now fixed as the centered half-atom
\[
  g_{\delta,t_0,0}(\xi)=\Lambda_\delta(\xi)\rho_{t_0}(\xi),
\]
with exact norms and core-mass bounds written in Section~\ref{sec:Main-closure}.
On the pilot compact $K=0.2$ and the sparse dictionary $J=\{0,1\}$ with
$\Delta=0.15$, the active prime set is only $\{\xi_2,\xi_3\}$, so for
$\delta<0.0124$ all prime collisions vanish and the finite-symbol criterion
reduces to a pure Archimedean comparison $\alpha_0>2|\alpha_1|$.

\subsection*{Archimedean bridge}
\emph{(A3) Archimedean Toeplitz barrier.} The Toeplitz component $T_M[P_A]$ in the Rayleigh decomposition of $Q^\star(t;\Phi_{B,t})$ is bounded from below by the explicit uniform Archimedean floor $c_*$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:uniform-arch-floor}), up to the controllable Lipschitz loss $C\,\omega_{P_A}(1/(2M))$. Szeg\H{o}--B\"ottcher asymptotics together with an explicit modulus of continuity for $P_A$ yield
\[
  \lambda_{\min}\!\big(T_M[P_A]\big)\ \ge\ c_*\ -\ C\,\omega_{P_A}\!\Big(\frac{1}{2M}\Big),
\]
as developed in Section~\ref{sec:A3}.

\subsection*{Prime contraction}
\emph{(RKHS) Uniform prime contraction without tables.} The prime contribution is encoded by the RKHS operator $T_P^{\mathrm{RKHS}}(t)$ supported on the nodes $\xi_n=\frac{\log n}{2\pi}$; in the Weil functional we use the one-sided weights $w_Q(n)=2\Lambda(n)/\sqrt{n}$, while in the RKHS analysis we keep the undoubled operator weights $w_{\mathrm{RKHS}}(n)=\Lambda(n)/\sqrt{n}$. Section~\ref{sec:rkhs-prime-cap} develops a tables-free cap from the closed form $\rho(t)$. For the main A3 bridge we use the single-scale evaluation at $t=t_{\mathrm{crit}}=3/20$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:uniform-prime-cap}).

\subsection*{Target-cone correction}
The target-cone audit of the current reset has one hard outcome: the broad cone
\[
  B_K=\{\Phi\in C_c(\mathbb R): \operatorname{supp}\Phi\subset[-K,K],\ \Phi\ \text{even}\}
\]
is too wide to serve as the public RH target. The classical Weil criterion is quadratic / positive-definite in nature, so the corrected local object is
\[
  \widetilde{\psi}(x):=\overline{\psi(-x)},
  \qquad
  \mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}
  := \overline{\operatorname{cone}\{\psi * \widetilde{\psi}:\ \psi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R),\ \operatorname{supp}\psi\subset[-K/2,K/2]\}}^{\|\cdot\|_\infty},
\]
and the global target is $\mathcal W^{\mathrm{pd}}=\varinjlim_{K>0}\mathcal W_K^{\mathrm{pd}}$.

\subsection*{Compact extension}
\emph{(A1$'$ vs. A1-pd).} The shifted density theorem A1$'$ is naturally a theorem on the broad restriction cone
\[
  R_K = C^+_{\mathrm{even}}([-K,K]).
\]
It is still useful as auxiliary approximation technology, but it no longer feeds the RH mainline directly. The new theorem package is: A1-pd on the dense autocorrelation side, exact packet-Rayleigh on that same packet side, and then `PSD-pd` as the single live knife-edge. Once packet-kernel positivity is closed, continuity from A2 gives the local closure step, and the LF tail becomes routine.

\subsection*{Outline of the proof}

Combining the Toeplitz barrier and the uniform RKHS cap yields, on the centered bridge family,
\[
  \lambda_{\min}\!\big(T_M[P_A]-T_P^{\mathrm{Ray}}(t,M)\big)
  \ \ge\ c_*\ -\ C\,\omega_{P_A}\!\Big(\frac{1}{2M}\Big)\ -\ \rho(t).
\]
Selecting $M\ge M_0^{\mathrm{unif}}$ gives $C\,\omega_{P_A}(1/(2M))\le c_*/2$, and
choosing $t=t_{\mathrm{crit}}=3/20$ yields $\rho(t_{\mathrm{crit}})\le c_*/4$, so
\[
  \lambda_{\min}\!\big(T_M[P_A]-T_P^{\mathrm{Ray}}(t,M)\big)\ \ge\ \frac{c_*}{4}\ >\ 0.
\]
Hence the analytic core already produces a centered positivity mechanism. The
remaining work is now organized in two layers. The primary layer is operator
theoretic: build the generalized form-pair bridge
\[
  \textup{H1} \Longrightarrow \textup{H2} \Longrightarrow \textup{H3} \Longrightarrow \textup{H4}
\]
from our finite Hermitian block to Suzuki's RH-equivalent nondegeneracy
criterion. The fallback layer keeps the packet package `P1--P6` and the
finite-dictionary `P7` criterion on each admissible packet block:
nonnegativity of the finite symbol $S_J(\theta)=A_J(\theta)-P_J(\theta)$,
concretely driven by coefficient bounds on $\alpha_m$ and $\beta_m$, with
Poisson-regularized finite symbols used only as a verification device and a
fallback full-kernel operator package kept in reserve. The compact scalar
package remains only as a rejected diagnostic reduction.
The new pilot computation shows that this finite-dictionary criterion is not
vacuous: for the canonical centered half-atom and a sparse compact packet
dictionary one really does get $S_J\ge0$ from geometric prime avoidance and a
positive Archimedean gap.

\subsection*{What is new}

Two features distinguish the present work.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{A consistent centered positivity engine.} The Archimedean symbol is defined once via periodization of the Fej\'er$\times$heat window, and its Lipschitz modulus is proved directly for that same object, eliminating mixed definitions.
  \item \textbf{A corrected RH target and a cleaner operator pivot.} The draft now separates the broad ambient compact-support cone from the corrected positive-definite Weil cone, rejects the compact scalar route as a public mainline on nontrivial compacts, and isolates two honest survivors: the fallback packet-kernel theorem \textup{PSD-pd} on the Weil side, and the primary live Suzuki/Yoshida bridge \textup{H1} $\to$ \textup{H2} $\to$ \textup{H3} $\to$ \textup{H4} on the operator side.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Notation}
