# Full-Kernel PSD Frontier

Date: 2026-03-07

## Summary

The literal packet-level `Route P`

`exact packet sesquilinear identity -> prime-block PSD factorization or Hilbert lift -> Archimedean domination -> PSD-pd`

is no longer treated as a live theorem package.

The new packet-space audit shows:

- exact packet sesquilinear identity survives;
- standalone PSD factorization of the packet prime block is false on dense
  packet spaces containing an active node;
- the honest theorem target is therefore direct PSD of the full kernel
  `K_Q(\Psi,\Phi)=Q^\star(t;\Psi * \widetilde{\Phi})`.

## Exact surviving theorem shape

For a dense pre-packet space `\mathcal P_K \subset C_c([-K/2,K/2])`, the live
frontier is:

`[Q^\star(t;\Psi_i * \widetilde{\Psi_j})]_{i,j} \succeq 0`

for every finite packet family `\Psi_1,\dots,\Psi_N \in \mathcal P_K`.

Equivalent spectral form:

- the Toeplitz sequence `(\kappa_m)` is positive-definite on `\mathbb Z`;
- equivalently the full symbol `S_{g,\Delta}=A_{g,\Delta}-P_{g,\Delta}` is
  nonnegative in the Herglotz sense;
- in a regular symbol regime, a sufficient route is
  `A_{g,\Delta}(\theta) \ge P_{g,\Delta}(\theta)` for all `\theta`.

## Prime-block obstruction

The packet prime form

`K_P(\Psi,\Phi)=\sum_n w(n)(\Psi * \widetilde{\Phi})(\xi_n)`

is not PSD in general on translation packet spaces containing an active node.
So no honest mainline may continue to treat

`prime block PSD factorization or Hilbert lift`

as a public theorem target.

## Surviving strategy split

- `Herglotz/Bochner`: secondary diagnostic / equivalence language.
- Direct full-kernel route: primary constructive route.

The direct route now means:

1. exact packet sesquilinear identity;
2. prime-block obstruction;
3. full-symbol domination `A_{g,\Delta} \ge P_{g,\Delta}` or a new operator
   package for the full kernel;
4. `PSD-pd`.
