refinement, median and overlay code, without writes or spectral scans.

cells hide no same-guard positive candidate in their tiny pruned sectors,

full beta_decimal before publication; all161 executed adaptive refinements were
zeta, so no numeric result changed. Hermite and envelope paths already preserve

cached sources used GL256. Those labels were corrected to the actual arrays;
source paths/hashes and all numeric values were unchanged. The repair receipt
and future lookup rule are retained. These are provenance fixes, not new

This is a finite methodological/readback review, not proof admission,
