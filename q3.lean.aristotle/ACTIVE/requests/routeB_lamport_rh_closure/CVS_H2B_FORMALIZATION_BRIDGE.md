## 1. CvS main theorem + CF Toeplitz corollary (verbatim, with locations)

### 1a. Main theorem — Theorem 6.1 (p. 13), announced as Theorem 1.2 (p. 2)

> **Theorem 6.1.** *Let L > 0, and let 𝒟 be a real distribution on the interval
> [0, L]. Let Q be the quadratic form defined on trigonometric polynomials by (6).
> Assume that Q defines a lower-bounded essentially selfadjoint operator and that
> the minimum of its spectrum is a simple, isolated eigenvalue λ, with even
> eigenfunction¹ ξ. Then all the zeros of the entire function ξ̂(z), z ∈ ℂ, the
> Fourier transform of ξ lie on the real line.*
>
> (footnote ¹: *i.e. invariant under the symmetry x ↦ L − x of [0, L]*)

Abstract phrasing (p. 1), same content: lowest spectral value λ simple, isolated,
even eigenfunction ξ̃ ⇒ all zeros of ξ̃̂(z) (Fourier transform of ξ̃) are real.

### 1b. The Carathéodory–Fejér corollary — Corollary 1.1 (p. 2)

> **Corollary 1.1.** *Let T ∈ M_{n+1}(ℂ) be a Hermitian, positive semidefinite
> Toeplitz matrix of rank n, and let ξ ∈ ker T. Then all the zeros of the
> polynomial*
>
> P(z) := Σ_{j=0}^{n} ξ_j z^j
>
> *lie on the unit circle.*

This is a direct corollary of the **Carathéodory–Fejér 1911 structure theorem**
(p. 2 top): a Hermitian PSD Toeplitz matrix `T` of rank `r` factors as
`T = V D V*` with `V` a Vandermonde matrix built from distinct points
`z₁,…,z_r ∈ 𝕋` (unit circle) and `D = diag(α₁,…,α_r)`, `α_k > 0`.

### 1c. Finite-dimensional real analogue actually used for the RH connection

Corollary 1.1 is proved *twice*: once C*-algebraically (§2, Prop 2.1/2.2), and once
by a purely linear-algebra route (§5) that is the one Q3 mirrors. The §5 statements:

> **Theorem 5.6 (p. 11).** *Let Q be a real symmetric positive matrix of the form
> (11) with one dimensional kernel which is even with respect to γ. Let ξ ∈ ker Q.
> (i) All the roots of the following polynomial are real:*
>
> P(s) = Σ_{k∈{−N,…,N}} ξ_k × ( ∏_{j∈{−N,…,N}, j≠k} (j − s) ).   … (21)
>
> *(ii) The Fourier transform ξ̂(z) of the function ξ(x) := Σ ξ_k exp(2πikx) …
> is entire and has all its zeros on the real line.*

> **Proposition 5.10 (p. 12).** *Let D have simple spectrum and let Q be a real
> symmetric positive matrix of the form (13) with one dimensional even kernel.
> Let ξ ∈ ker Q. Then all the roots of P(s) = Σ_k ξ_k ∏_{j≠k}(λ_j − s) are real.*   … (24)

Matrix normal form (11), p. 8, Prop 4.2 — **this is the exact shape Q3's `q_{ij}` has**:

> q_{i,i} = a_i,  ∀i;   q_{i,j} = (b_i − b_j)/(i − j), ∀j ≠ i,   with a_{−i}=a_i, b_{−i}=−b_i.  … (11)

## 2. Proof skeleton of the CF corollary (numbered steps)

Two independent proofs. Q3 mirrors the **§5 linear-algebra route**, not the §2
C*-algebra route. Both are given so the bridge is unambiguous.

### Route α — C*-algebraic (Corollary 1.1 via §2). NOT what Q3 targets.
α1. (Prop 2.1, p. 3) From PSD Toeplitz `T` of rank `n` with `(a_j) ∈ ker T`,
    build the involutive algebra `A = ℂ[X,X⁻¹]`, ideal `J = P·ℂ[X]` generated by
    `P(X)=Σa_jX^j`; monomials `X^j (j=0..n−1)` are a basis of `A/J`.
α2. There is a unique linear form `φ` on `A/J` with `φ(X^j)=c_j`, and (using the
    Toeplitz rows + `(a_j)∈ker T`) `φ` is **positive** on the *-algebra `A/J`.
α3. (Prop 2.2, p. 3) `φ` positive ⇒ positive measure on `U(1)` via GNS; supported
    on the `n` eigenvalues of the unitary `π(X)`; since `P ∈ J = ker π`, the roots
    of `P` are these eigenvalues ⇒ **all on the unit circle**.
α4. (Remark 2.3) If the eigenvalue is *not* simple (kernel dim > 1) the conclusion
    holds only for the **intersection** of the zero-sets — "radical of the quadratic
    form". This is exactly why the **simplicity hypothesis is essential**.

### Route β — finite-dim real spectral theorem (§5). THIS is the Q3 target.
β1. (Lemma 5.1(i), p. 9) Grading involution `γ` with `γ(e_j)=e_{−j}`, `γ²=Id`,
    `Qγ=γQ`.
β2. (Lemma 5.1(ii) / eq (16), p. 9) `D` diagonal with `D(e_j)=j e_j`, `Dγ=−γD`, and
    the commutator `D Q − Q D = |β⟩⟨η| − |η⟩⟨β|`, with `β=Σb_j e_j`, `η=Σe_j`.
β3. (Lemma 5.2(i), p. 9) Assume `Q ≥ 0`, `Qξ=0`, `γξ=ξ`, `⟨ξ|η⟩=1`. Then
    `Q D ξ = −β`.
β4. (Lemma 5.2(ii), p. 9) `D′ := D − |Dξ⟩⟨η|` is **self-adjoint with respect to the
    inner product defined by Q** (i.e. `⟨D′f | g⟩_Q = ⟨f | D′g⟩_Q`).
β5. (Theorem 5.6 proof, p. 11) `D′ξ = 0`. `D′` therefore descends to an operator
    `D″` on the **separated quotient** `E = (ℝ^{2N+1}, Q)/radical(Q)`; the radical is
    the one-dimensional `ℝ∙ξ` (this uses `ker Q` one-dimensional, i.e. **simple**).
β6. The induced form on `E` is a genuine (positive-definite) Euclidean inner product,
    `D″` is self-adjoint on `E`, so the **real spectral theorem** gives real
    eigenvalues `λ_j` with orthonormal eigenvectors `v_j`.
β7. Lifting to `w_j` and using `D′w_j = λ_j w_j + s_j ξ`, in the basis `{ξ, w_j}` the
    matrix of `D′` is **triangular**: `Det(D′ − s) = −s · ∏_j (λ_j − s)`.
β8. (Lemma 5.3 / eq (18), p. 10) Independently, the matrix-determinant lemma gives
    `Det(D′ − s) = −s · Det(D − s) · Σ_{j} ξ_j/(s − j)`, and
    `Det(D′ − s)=0 ⟺ Σ_j ξ_j/(s − j) = 0`. Comparing with β7 ⇒ the roots of
    `P(s)` in (21) are exactly the real `λ_j` ⇒ **all roots of P(s) real**.
β9. (Theorem 5.6(ii), p. 12) Fourier transform:
    `ξ̂(z) = 2e^{−iz/2} sin(z/2) · Σ_{j} ξ_j/(z − 2πj)`; the `sin(z/2)` zeros cancel
    the poles at `2πj` where `ξ_j≠0`, remaining zeros are roots of `P(z/2π)` ⇒
    **all zeros of ξ̂ real**. (Infinite-dim Theorem 6.1 then follows by Hurwitz +
    approximation, §6.)

Key structural fact (Remark 5.4, p. 10; Remark 4.3 / §7): `Q` of form (11)/(13) is
the **second Gâteaux derivative of the spectral action** `tr f(D+tA)` — this is the
"echo of the spectral action" and eq (18) is an ordinary **Lagrange interpolation**
polynomial. This is why divided differences (§7) appear.

## 4. The exact MISSING lemma(s) to formalize CvS's CF corollary in Q3

Informal Lean-style signatures. These are the load-bearing gaps; none are written.

### M1 — Q-self-adjoint + Q positive-definite ⇒ real charpoly zeros  (bridges β4 ⊗ β8d)
This is THE keystone: it welds `rankOneCorrection_weightedSymmetric` to
`zerosRealOn_of_hermitian_charpoly_mul`. Congruence `Q = SᵀS` (Cholesky/`Q.sqrt`),
then `S · D′ · S⁻¹` is genuinely symmetric, so its (= `D′`'s) charpoly has real roots.

```
theorem realSpectrum_of_weightedSymmetric_posDef
    {n : Type*} [Fintype n] [DecidableEq n]
    (Q D' : Matrix n n ℝ)
    (hQpd : Q.PosDef)                      -- genuine positive-definite form
    (hsym : Q * D' = D'.transpose * Q) :   -- D' self-adjoint w.r.t. ⟨·,·⟩_Q
    ZerosRealOn Set.univ (fun z => (D'.map ofReal).charpoly.eval z)
```
Needs: `Matrix.PosDef` (HAVE in Mathlib) + a symmetric square root / congruence to a
`Matrix.IsHermitian` (partial Mathlib support in `LinearAlgebra/Matrix/PosDef.lean`;
the "congruence preserves spectrum-reality" step must be assembled).

### M2 — degenerate form: descend to the Q-separated quotient with a PosDef metric  (β5b+β5c+β6)
CvS's `Q` is only PSD with `Qξ=0`, so M1 cannot apply on the nose; one must pass to
`E = ℝ^n / radical(Q)`. Two sub-gaps:

```
-- (a) simple lowest eigenvalue ⇒ radical is exactly the calibration line
theorem radical_eq_span_xi_of_simple
    (Q : Matrix n n ℝ) (xi : n → ℝ)
    (hQ : Q.PosSemidef) (hker1 : rank of ker (Q.mulVecLin) = 1)
    (hxi : Matrix.mulVec Q xi = 0) (hxi0 : xi ≠ 0) :
    LinearMap.ker (Q.mulVecLin) = ℝ ∙ xi
-- (b) induced form on (n→ℝ)/ℝ∙xi is PosDef and D' descends self-adjointly
theorem inducedForm_posDef_and_quotient_selfadjoint
    (Q D' : Matrix n n ℝ) (xi eta : n → ℝ) …
    : (induced form on E).PosDef ∧
      (rankOneCorrectionQuotientEnd … is self-adjoint for that form)
```
The `RankOneCorrectionQuotientDescent` file gives the *plain* quotient `(n→ℝ)/ℝ∙ξ`
(HAVE), but with **no metric**; M2 must equip it with the induced PosDef form and
re-prove self-adjointness there. This is where the **simplicity hypothesis (H2a)**
is literally consumed.

### M3 — determinant → Lagrange form, then real roots of P(s)  (β7+β8c)
Turn the adjugate identity already proved into eq (18)/Lemma 5.3, and identify with (21).

```
theorem det_rankOneCorrection_eq_lagrange   -- eq (18)
    (D : Matrix n n ℝ) (xi eta : n → ℝ) (s : ℝ) (hDdiag : D = diagonal lam) …
    : (rankOneCorrection D xi eta - s • 1).det
        = (- s) * (D - s • 1).det * ∑ j, xi j / (s - lam j)

theorem P_zerosReal_of_charpoly_real       -- Lem 5.3(i) + Thm 5.6(i)
    … (hreal : ZerosRealOn univ (charpoly of D'')) :
    ZerosRealOn Set.univ (fun s => ∑ k, xi k * ∏ j ≠ k, (lam j - s))
```

`cramer_eq_adjugate_mulVec` (both already used), `LinearAlgebra/Lagrange.lean`,
`LinearAlgebra/Vandermonde.lean`.

### M4 — (only for the full Corollary 1.1 / Theorem 6.1) Fourier + Hurwitz  (β9)

## 5. What is ASSUMED vs PROVED (confirming simple+even is an INPUT)

- **ASSUMED (input, = H2a, OPEN):** "the minimum of its spectrum is a **simple,
  isolated** eigenvalue λ, with **even** eigenfunction ξ" (Thm 6.1). In the matrix
  layer this is "Q … with **one dimensional kernel which is even** with respect to γ"
  (Thm 5.6) and "`D` has **simple spectrum**" (Prop 5.10). Simplicity is essential —
  Remark 2.3 (p. 3) states that with a higher-dim kernel one gets reality only for the
  **intersection** of the eigenfunctions' zero-sets ("radical of the quadratic form").
- **PROVED (H2b engine):** given that input, all zeros of `P(s)` (finite) / `ξ̂(z)`
  (infinite) are real.

⇒ H2b is **conditional on H2a**. Do not report H2b as closed.

## 6. Verdict

**Formalizable by extending the existing scaffold — YES, for the finite-matrix layer
(Theorem 5.6(i) / Prop 5.10), and it does NOT require Toeplitz / Carathéodory–Fejér
machinery.** Q3's Route B deliberately follows CvS's §5 real-spectral-theorem route,
which *bypasses* the §2 C*-algebraic CF proof. The scaffold already owns the two ends
of the argument:

1. `rankOneCorrection_weightedSymmetric` — `D′` is Q-self-adjoint (β4); and
2. `zerosRealOn_of_hermitian_charpoly_mul` — a genuinely Hermitian matrix's charpoly
   has real zeros (β8d).

The missing middle is **one keystone lemma M1** ("self-adjoint w.r.t. a positive-
definite form ⇒ real spectrum", via congruence `Q=SᵀS`), plus the **degenerate-
quotient handling M2** (where simplicity/H2a is consumed) and the **Lagrange
identification M3**. All three are ordinary linear algebra reachable from Mathlib
primitives already in use here.

**Mathlib gap assessment (searched the pinned copy at
`q3.lean.aristotle/.lake/packages/mathlib`):**

- **Carathéodory–Fejér Toeplitz structure theorem: NOT in Mathlib.** The only
  "Carathéodory" is the measure-theoretic extension theorem
  (`MeasureTheory/…`, `Probability/…`); the only "Toeplitz" hits are the
  **Hellinger–Toeplitz** theorem (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Symmetric.lean`) and a
  Toeplitz–Hausdorff numerical-range mention — neither is the PSD-Toeplitz /
  Vandermonde-decomposition result. No `Matrix.IsToeplitz`, no
  `PosSemidef Toeplitz ⇒ Vandermonde` factorization.
- **"Zeros of P on the unit circle" for PSD Toeplitz (Corollary 1.1 directly): NOT in
  Mathlib.** Would require the CF theorem above.
- **BUT Q3 does not need either**, because it takes the §5 route. The primitives that
  route needs **are** present: `Matrix.PosDef` / `PosSemidef`
  (`LinearAlgebra/Matrix/PosDef.lean`), `Matrix.IsHermitian.spectrum_eq_image_range`
  (real eigenvalues, `Analysis/Matrix/Spectrum.lean` — already used by the scaffold),
  `det_add_replicateCol_mul_replicateRow` + `cramer_eq_adjugate_mulVec` (matrix
  determinant lemma, already used), `LinearAlgebra/Vandermonde.lean`,
  `LinearAlgebra/Lagrange.lean`, and quotient-module infrastructure
  (`LinearAlgebra/Quotient/Basic.lean`, already used).
- The **one primitive not packaged** is "a matrix self-adjoint with respect to a
  positive-definite inner product has real spectrum (is congruent to a symmetric
  matrix)". It is *derivable* from `Matrix.PosDef` + a symmetric square root but is not
  a named Mathlib lemma — this is exactly gap **M1**.

**Bottom line for the caller:** extend the scaffold, don't wait on Toeplitz/CF Mathlib.
Priority order for H2b (finite layer): **M1 → M2 → M3** (then optionally M4 for the
analytic Theorem 6.1). And keep the conditional flag: H2b stays OPEN until H2a
(simple + even lowest eigenvalue) is supplied.
