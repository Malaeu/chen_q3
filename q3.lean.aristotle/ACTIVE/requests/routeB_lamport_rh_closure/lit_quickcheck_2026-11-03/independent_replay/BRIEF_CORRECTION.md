# Separate correction: where to search Ψ minima

The pinned brief remains unchanged. For the stated prime-side formula, at t=log(p^k),

`Ψ′(t+)−Ψ′(t−)=−log(p)/sqrt(p^k)`.

The derivative jumps downward. Such a point may be a local maximum; there is no general minimum-at-the-left-of-every-hinge rule. Check both one-sided event values, endpoints, and stationary points inside every smooth interval. Q4 does so. Also Ψ(t)→0 as t↓0: the minimum among nonzero sampled candidates must not be called a uniform positive lower bound on (0,T].

This is a correction to the diagnostic procedure, not an RH assertion. External Q4 formulas and proof claims still require the unavailable source.
