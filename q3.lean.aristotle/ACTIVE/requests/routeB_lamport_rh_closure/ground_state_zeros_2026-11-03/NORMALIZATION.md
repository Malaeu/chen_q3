# Ground, Fourier and zero conventions

DIAGNOSTIC_NEVER_A_PROOF. This packet does not alter D0 or Route B state.

## Two ground-state questions

The literal window is [-L/2,L/2], L=log m, N=m. The orthonormal even basis is psi0=1/sqrt(L), psin=sqrt(2/L)(-1)^n cos(2pi n x/L); the odd basis uses sqrt(2/L)(-1)^n sin(2pi n x/L). Functions are zero outside the window. The even restricted ground and the global ground of the direct sum of both parity blocks are reported separately. Calling an even-block vector even is not a test of global ground parity.

The even simplicity gap is lambda_even1-lambda_even0. The global gap is the difference of the first two values in the merged ordered even/odd low pairs. The parity gap is lambda_odd0-lambda_even0. For a macroscopic negative ground, the second eigenvalue may remain at an arithmetic floor while its absolute gap from the ground is well resolved; its printed sign and second vector are not then claimed resolved.

The vectors have physical L2 norm one. Root locations do not depend on a scalar normalization against H(t)=4xi(1/2-it). No global amplitude ratio is used to infer zero recovery. Relative-to-local-amplitude and actual root displacement are different objects.

## Entire Fourier transform

Fourier convention: F(z)=integral f(x)exp(izx)dx. For the even coefficient vector c,

    F(z)=2sin(zL/2)/(sqrt(L)z) * [c0+sqrt(2) sum_{n=1}^m cn z^2/(z^2-omega_n^2)],
    omega_n=2pi n/L.

After a common denominator this has a degree-m numerator in u=(z/Omega)^2, Omega=2pi m/L. For an odd vector the factor is 2i sqrt(2/L)sin(zL/2), multiplying sum cn omega_n/(z^2-omega_n^2), with numerator degree at most m-1. A stable sinc formula gives the entire continuation at all apparent poles.

Internal harmonics are not automatic zeros: F_even(omega_n)=sqrt(L/2)(-1)^n cn and F_odd(omega_n)=i sqrt(L/2)(-1)^n cn. The origin also needs continuation: F_even(0)=sqrt(L)c0; every odd transform has its symmetry zero at0. By contrast every external harmonic omega_k,k>m, is an exact structural zero of both transforms. Full-transform counts and numerator-only counts therefore differ, even in the true world.

    T=(1+(1+log L)/(4L))*Omega.

## Counts, matching and precision

High-vector root discovery uses the numerator polynomial alone, without source-zero seeds. Forced external roots are then added exactly. One-to-one assignment uses Euclidean cost; discrete pairing is selected numerically and distances are recomputed in multiprecision. The raw assigned count is not the number of close matches. Reports separately retain close counts at1e-2,1e-6,1e-12, both-side unmatched counts, and raw maximum assignment distance. Numerator-only matching prevents structural lattice roots from disguising missing source zeros. Spacing-normalized distances use the nearest source point in the complex plane.

Native MPFR eigenvalue ordering, residuals and source precision repeats are numerical diagnostics, not outward-rounded source enclosures. The analytic digamma source keeps every signed arithmetic coefficient, the full archimedean tail, both parity-specific pole contributions and the planted update. It is regressed independently against both parity quadrature formulas and the pinned even source at high m. No zero-value Gram matrix or RH assumption enters the source assembly.

HIGH low pairs keep native ordered eigenvalue computation throughout. Reviewed LOW-only shifted refinement may replace a repeated native solve: it uses independently rebuilt/rounded LOW source entries, the ordered HIGH seed, a measured LOW/HIGH point-matrix perturbation gate and an explicit residual check; failures fall back to native LOW. Its LOW second eigenpair is NOT_COMPUTED and only a Weyl comparison range is retained. These fields are not disguised as an independent LOW lambda1 solve. Reviewed zeta-even numerator continuation is likewise accepted only after all disjoint pole-free Arb sign brackets and the full polynomial degree account for every root; otherwise global Flint isolation is used. Previous numerator roots are numerical starting points, never source-zeta seeds.

Polynomial arithmetic replay at a fixed rounded vector does not establish convergence of the ground transform. Low/high source/eigenvector repeats are retained and their actual transform roots compared. Full independent low polynomial isolation is used in the selected controls; bulk replay uses high-root-seeded Newton with uniqueness/convergence checks and fallback. Root-set count claims distinguish complete isolation from replayed-only counts. Generic L2 perturbation bounds can be very pessimistic in a nearly radical cluster and are not silently replaced by empirical root stability. Reference and output floors are retained; a zero displayed displacement is not an exact source identity.

Plots and compact tables use logarithms of multiprecision decimal strings. Tiny eigenvalues around1e-505 must not be converted to binary64 before taking their logarithms.
