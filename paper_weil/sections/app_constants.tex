Positivity of $\Phi$ on $[0,\infty)$: $h(u)<0$ only for $u<\sqrt{3/(2\pi)}=0.691$, so for $x\ge0$ every term $h(ne^x)$ of \eqref{eq:Phi} is positive;
for $x<0$ use evenness. Hence $\Phi>0$ everywhere.

Envelope constants. Put $y=\pi n^2e^{2x}$, so that $e^{x/2}=\pi^{-1/4}n^{-1/2}y^{1/4}$ and $4h(ne^x)e^{x/2}\cdot e^{x/2}\cdot\pi^{1/4}n^{1/2}y^{-1/4}$
is a polynomial times $e^{-y}$: precisely, $\Phi(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}e^{x/2}P_0(\pi n^2e^{2x})e^{-\pi n^2e^{2x}}$ with $P_0(y)=4y^2-6y$, and differentiating
in $x$ gives $\Phi^{(j)}(x)=\sum_ne^{x/2}P_j(\pi n^2e^{2x})e^{-\pi n^2e^{2x}}$ with $P_{j+1}(y)=\tfrac12P_j(y)+2y\bigl(P_j'(y)-P_j(y)\bigr)$, so
$P_1(y)=-8y^3+30y^2-15y$ and $P_2(y)=16y^4-112y^3+165y^2-\tfrac{75}2y$. Writing $e^{x/2}=\pi^{-1/4}n^{-1/2}y^{1/4}$, bounding each monomial by its maximum
against half of the Gaussian, $y^{r+1/4}e^{-y/2}\le(2(r+\tfrac14)/e)^{r+1/4}$, keeping the other half $e^{-y/2}\le e^{-\frac\pi2e^{2|x|}}e^{-\frac\pi2(n^2-1)e^{2|x|}}$
and summing the $n\ge2$ terms geometrically ($e^{2|x|}\ge1$, ratio $\le e^{-3\pi/2}$) gives
$|\Phi^{(j)}(x)|\le AM_j\,e^{-\frac\pi2e^{2|x|}}$ with $M_j=\frac{\pi^{-1/4}}{A(1-e^{-3\pi/2})}\sum_r|P_{j,r}|\bigl(2(r+\tfrac14)/e\bigr)^{r+1/4}$, i.e.\
$M_0=23.910$, $M_1=325.43$, $M_2=6011.8$ (the constants are crude and only their finiteness is used).
