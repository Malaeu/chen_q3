\section{What cannot be a proof, and what remains}\label{sec:ledger}

We list the proof shapes excluded by theorems in this paper, with the reason. Each entry was, at some point, a live plan.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Separate estimates of the three parts of \eqref{eq:Q}.} The archimedean part alone is unbounded below on tests of growing support
 (its constant tends to $-\infty$ with the window length), and the prime part alone is not nonnegative; the mean-prime surrogate $\Lam(n)\to1$ makes $\Q$ strictly negative on
 explicit tests. Only the coupled form has a chance.
\item \textbf{Local sum-of-squares certificates} (finite stencils, edges, second differences, prime rectangles, any diameter, any
 cardinality): \Cref{thm:nominorant}, \Cref{cor:noCSS}. The obstruction is the translated radical, and it persists for continuous
 families of stencils.
\item \textbf{A uniform margin on a dense family:} \Cref{prop:trade}.
\item \textbf{Pointwise majorisation of the negative part} (Schur-type bounds): \Cref{rem:schur}; this entry is a heuristic, not a theorem of this paper.
\item \textbf{Plane-wave positivity as a sufficient condition:} the two-frequency kernel is not diagonal; and without RH the
 zero-side squares are complex (\Cref{sec:gs}).
\item \textbf{Finite-window premises} (bounded relative error along the wide schedule, positivity of all computed bottoms):
 RH-equivalent or upper bounds only (\Cref{sec:windows}).
\item \textbf{Deformation along the de Bruijn--Newman flow.} In the logarithmic variable the flow is $f_0\mapsto e^{tx^2/4}f_0$
 (\Cref{thm:FPhi}); for $t\ne0$ there is no Euler product, hence no prime side, and the known results go the other way:
 $\Lambda\ge0$ \cite{RodgersTao2018} says that RH, if true, has no margin.
\item \textbf{Import from other equivalents.} Among the equivalents surveyed in \cite{Broughan2023}, the unconditional partial results
 (Nicolas' oscillation theorems \cite{Nicolas1983}, $\Lambda\ge0$, $\Lambda\le0.22$ \cite{Polymath15}, hyperbolicity of Jensen
 polynomials for $d\le8$ \cite{GORZ2019}) either point in the barely-true direction or concern only the archimedean moments of
 $f_0$; none sees the prime atoms inside a quadratic form (cf.\ \cite{Farmer2020}).
\end{enumerate}

Two external facts corroborate the zero-margin picture from outside our construction: $\Lambda\ge0$ and Nicolas' theorem that
his inequality, if RH fails, holds and fails infinitely often. Both say that no equivalent of RH comes with a uniform positive margin.

\begin{problem}\label{prob:DOM}
Prove \eqref{eq:DOM}. Equivalently, find a representation of $\Q(f_0s)$ as a manifestly nonnegative expression whose every positive
factor annihilates the entire translated radical $\{U_qf_0\}_{q\in\R}$ and all $\Xi$-multiples $\{f_0*h\}$.
\end{problem}

Two representations are not excluded by the results above. (R1) A \emph{nonlocal} positive factorisation acting on $f_0s$ (a global
integral, as in \Cref{ex:control}), with its annihilation of the radical proved before its sign is estimated; the object to audit is
the full two-frequency kernel, not its diagonal. (R2) Cutoff-dependent finite Gram certificates $K_m+e_mI\succeq0$ with an
independently proved allowance $e_m\ge0$, $e_m\to0$, and a proved recovery of every fixed test from the windows; such a family is
not a fixed positive-series identity and escapes \Cref{cor:noCSS}, but its universal quantifier over $m$ cannot be supplied by
any finite list of cells.
