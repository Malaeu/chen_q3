\section{The Weil form in the logarithmic variable}\label{sec:setup}

Throughout, $x=\log u$ is the logarithmic variable on the multiplicative group, tests are complex-valued, and
\[
 \widehat g(z)=\int_{\R} g(x)\,e^{-izx}\,dx,\qquad (U_qg)(x)=g(x-q).
\]
We write $\Xi(z)=\xi(\tfrac12+iz)$ for the centred Riemann $\Xi$ function, $\Lam$ for the von Mangoldt function,
$w_n=\Lam(n)/\sqrt n$, and $Z(\Xi)$ for the multiset of zeros of $\Xi$.

\begin{definition}[source form]\label{def:Q}
For $g\in C_c^\infty(\R;\C)$ put $H(g)=\|g\|_2^2$, $C_g(t)=\Re\int\overline{g(x)}\,g(x+t)\,dx$, $A_\pm(g)=\int g(x)e^{\pm x/2}dx$,
\[
 a(t)=\frac{e^{-t/2}}{1-e^{-2t}},\qquad c_A=\gamma+\log 8\pi+\frac{\pi}{2},\qquad
 \D(g)=\int_0^\infty a(t)\,\|g(\cdot+t)-g\|_2^2\,dt ,
\]
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Q}
 \Q(g)=\D(g)-c_A\,H(g)+2\Re\bigl(A_+(g)\overline{A_-(g)}\bigr)-2\sum_{n\ge2}w_n\,C_g(\log n).
\end{equation}
$\B(g,v)$ denotes the Hermitian polarisation of $\Q$ (antilinear in the first slot).
\end{definition}

The three groups of terms are the archimedean part (the translation energy $\D$ and the constant $c_A$), the pole
part, and the prime part. For compactly supported tests the explicit formula of Weil \cite{Weil1952}, in the form of
\cite[\S2]{BOMBIERI-WEILQF-2000} transported to the logarithmic variable and polarised, reads
\begin{equation}\label{eq:EF}
 \B(g,v)=\sum_{z\in Z(\Xi)}\overline{\widehat g(\bar z)}\,\widehat v(z),\qquad
 \Q(g)=\sum_{z\in Z(\Xi)}\overline{\widehat g(\bar z)}\,\widehat g(z)\qquad(g,v\in C_c^\infty(\R;\C)),
\end{equation}
so that $\Q(g)=\sum_z|\widehat g(z)|^2$ under the Riemann hypothesis. The normalisation of \eqref{eq:Q} was checked
against \eqref{eq:EF} numerically on the canonical test of \Cref{sec:canonical} (\Cref{app:constants}). Weil's criterion
\cite{Weil1952,BOMBIERI-WEILQF-2000} is the statement that RH holds if and only if $\Q(g)\ge0$ for all
$g\in C_c^\infty(\R;\C)$. Statements that use \eqref{eq:EF} are marked as such; everything else is proved from \eqref{eq:Q} alone.

\paragraph{Control space.} Let $\Xf$ be the completion of $C_c^\infty(\R;\C)$ for the norm
$\|g\|_{\Xf}^2=\|e^{|x|}g\|_2^2+\D(g)$; concretely, $\Xf$ is the space of measurable $g$ with $e^{|x|}g\in L^2$ and $\D(g)<\infty$
(the two conditions are preserved under the mollifications and cutoffs that approximate such $g$ by $C_c^\infty$ functions). Each term
of \eqref{eq:Q} is bounded on $\Xf$: $|\D(g,v)|\le\D(g)^{1/2}\D(v)^{1/2}$ by Cauchy--Schwarz; $|c_A|\,|\langle g,v\rangle|\le|c_A|\,\|g\|_{\Xf}\|v\|_{\Xf}$;
$|A_\pm(g)|\le\|e^{|x|}g\|_2\,\|e^{\pm x/2-|x|}\|_2=\tfrac2{\sqrt3}\|e^{|x|}g\|_2$, so the pole term is bounded by $\tfrac83\|g\|_{\Xf}\|v\|_{\Xf}$;
and $|C_{g,v}(t)|\le e^{-|t|}\|e^{|x|}g\|_2\|e^{|x|}v\|_2$ with $\sum_n\log n\,n^{-3/2}=-\zeta'(3/2)=3.9325$ bounds the prime part by
$7.87\,\|g\|_{\Xf}\|v\|_{\Xf}$. Altogether
\[
 |\B(g,v)|\le C_{\Xf}\|g\|_{\Xf}\|v\|_{\Xf},\qquad C_{\Xf}=|c_A|+14\ (\text{valid; the sum of the four bounds is }|c_A|+11.54),
\]
and $\B$ extends by continuity to $\Xf$. Translations act boundedly on $\Xf$, with $\|e^{|x|}U_qg\|_2\le e^{|q|}\|e^{|x|}g\|_2$.

\paragraph{Finite windows.} Restricting $\Q$ to tests supported in $[-\tfrac12\log m,\tfrac12\log m]$ and expanding in
the prolate basis of Connes--Consani--Moscovici \cite{CCM-ZST-2025} (the prolate operator and its relation to the zeros is the
subject of \cite{ConnesMoscovici2021}) produces the finite matrices $K(m,N)$ studied there
(and, in additive coordinates, in \cite{XUEFENGZHU-2026}). Two facts from \cite{CCM-ZST-2025} fix the logical status of
everything finite in this paper: the bottom $\lambda_1(m,N)$ of $K(m,N)$ is an \emph{upper} bound for the infimum
$\mu_\lambda$ of $\Q$ on the window (their Prop.~3.4), and $\mu_\lambda\ge0$ for all $\lambda$ is equivalent to RH
(their Cor.~3.8, via the monotonicity $\lambda>\lambda'\Rightarrow\mu_\lambda\le\mu_{\lambda'}$). Positivity of $\Q$ on
small windows is a theorem \cite{YOSHIDA-HERMITIAN-1992,BOMBIERI-WEILQF-2000,CC-WEILPOS-2020}; the proved range stops at
support length $\log 2$, before the first prime atom enters.
