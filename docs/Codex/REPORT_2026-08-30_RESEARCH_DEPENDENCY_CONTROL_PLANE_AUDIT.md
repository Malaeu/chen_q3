## Result

The active workflow now treats a named theorem X as a candidate supplier for an
exact downstream consumer Y, not as a requirement by repetition. Every
reopenable debt records the actual consumer contract, necessity status, weaker
interfaces Z, exact Z-to-Y obligation, failure type, novelty probe and evidence.
Execution status and epistemic status are independent.

The audit covered canonical Codex/Proshka controls, all repository skills,
session entry and briefing, active request/state surfaces, generators and
migrators, semantic corpus selection, Route B bus, genealogy, consolidation,
corrections, INSIGHTS and archived/historical requests and verdicts. A broad
lexical scan produced 969 candidate hard-dependency lines; because most are
historical evidence, byte/source identity requirements or already frozen local
contracts, the regression gate is structural and lifecycle-aware rather than a
banned-word regex.

The original task was then re-read against the delivered tree after all six
specialist audits. The closure pass added the missing contextual rigid-language
scanner, deterministic registry projection, normal-P9 wiring, source-generator
checks, dispatch eligibility checks, and the live knowledge migration. Strict
session start now passes with `research_dependency=PASS` and zero migration
debt.

## Critical faults found and repaired

1. `RESEARCH_DEBT_CHALLENGE` had been presented as a new review kind, but
   Control v9 has no such call class. It is now explicitly a packet subtype;
   dispatch requires an independently eligible existing `EXPLORATION_REVIEW`.
2. The packet lacked `REQUEST_ID` and `BOUNDARY_ID`, so `review-plan` could
   never accept it. Both are now mandatory and byte-bound.
3. Proshka's active prompt self-selected every repository `OPEN` and wildcard
   mandate. It now acts only on one exact attached request selected by the
   registered plan in the living phase chat.
4. Proshka and Codex controls conflated no-source/no-progress/cost with a dead
   route. Both now require consumer-first dependency epistemics; mathematical
   death needs counterexample, incompatibility or formal impossibility evidence.
5. The semantic corpus embedded a dormant Step33 monitor and stale request
   payloads (about 4.69 MB), an obsolete no-creativity solver prompt and a dated
   active governor. These sources are now excluded at the corpus selector.
6. A stale workflow required a fresh Proshka chat for every proof transaction;
   it now points to the one-living-chat Control lifecycle.
7. The old orchestration addendum is explicitly superseded so its manual
   GitHub-harvest and owner-route-choice text cannot be mistaken for current
   authority.

## Gates and plants

`orchestrator/research_dependency_gate.py` verifies:

- the strict registry schema and evidence split;
- byte identity of all three active Proshka prompt mirrors;
- source-locked intake and consumer-first prompt markers;
- exclusion of stale semantic sources;
- rejection of no-source/cost/derivation as mathematical death;
- rejection of `PROVED_NECESSARY` without pinned evidence;
- acceptance of counterexample-backed, exactly scoped death.
- byte freshness of the deterministic semantic projection;
- contextual hard-X claims in active controls and their generators: a claim is
  accepted only beside its downstream consumer and minimal-interface rationale.
- authoritative top-level lifecycle rather than incidental nested `CLOSED`
  examples, with historical sections excluded locally rather than hiding a live
  document;
- actual `q3_docs_corpus.collect_sources()` membership, not merely the presence
  of exclusion strings in generator source.

The gate runs inside normal P9 rather than as an optional audit command. The
negative wiring plant proves that ordinary P9 fails closed when the dependency
gate fails. Mutation plants cover unjustified active prose, unjustified
generator output, justified consumer-first contracts and historical CLOSED
surfaces.

The challenge and briefing plants also bind request/boundary IDs, final LF,
deterministic bytes, lifecycle transitions and the no-auto-reopen boundary.

## Final invariant

No active control may require theorem X merely because X was named earlier.
Every live dependency begins at consumer Y, audits the weakest sufficient Z,
and records whether X is necessary or merely sufficient. `KILL_*` is an
execution result with exact scope; only proof of falsity or impossibility can
create mathematical death. This package changes no six-field phase key,
selects no Route B root and makes no RH claim.
