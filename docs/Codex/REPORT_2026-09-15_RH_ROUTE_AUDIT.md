## 1. What introducing V accomplished

Theorem T and sections 3--8 of FULL_SIGN_TRANSFER_AUDIT prove the two-way
all-test transfer Q>=0 iff V>=0. The named published Weil criterion then
links it to RH. The arithmetic Q includes both pole terms, the grouped
archimedean constant and the complete prime-power sum. Passing to V replaced
that explicit-formula bookkeeping with an integral in one complete source.
It did not remove these terms from the mathematical object or prove its sign.
The finite coefficient congruence avoids an unjustified globally bounded
inverse Fourier multiplier. The form norm and density limits are paid.

ANALYTIC_POSITIVITY_PROPAGATION A1--A2 shows all ranks on any nonempty open
interval suffice. It does not reduce all ranks to two ranks, a fixed grid,
or a cofinal tail of fixed-rank examples. This is a genuine domain reduction,
but its remaining all-rank statement is still equivalent to RH.

The precise answer to 'why V?' is therefore: exact access to full-source
translate integrals and local analytic propagation. The expected next gain
was a common positive representation. That next gain has not been obtained.

## 3. One worked mechanism that really uses the source

Put q_lambda(t)=lambda exp(-lambda t)1_(t>0). For increasing node lists,
the matrix of step indicators 1_(u_i>v_j) has determinant zero or one.
Positive row/column factors give det[q_lambda(u_i-v_j)]>=0.

Continuous Cauchy--Binet says that convolving two such kernels preserves
these ordered determinants: the determinant of the convolution is an integral
over ordered intermediate nodes of a product of two nonnegative determinants.
For r_N=*_(n<=N)(q_(pi*n^2)*q_(pi*n^2)), this gives every finite order.
The source-tail coupling gives ||r-r_N||_infinity<=2pi/N, so each fixed
minor converges to the full r minor. The arbitrary order is chosen BEFORE
this limit. This closes the all-order TN theorem for the actual r.

This is an honest example of 'proved property -> decisive mechanism -> result'.
It is not a new discovery; the accepted September 12 intake already contains it.
Its output is ordered additive minors, not Hermitian quadratic positivity.
The continuous convolution theorem does not say that log coordinates preserve
that output, or that a target-minus-carrier difference is positive.

The negative f0 control discriminates this input: its inverse source density
r0(t)=t^(-5/4)f0(log(t)/2) has
(log r0)''(t)=[3/4+(log t)/2+o(1)]/t^2>0 for large t, so it is not TN2.
This shows why r's property is informative. It does not make that property,
even together with a label 'theta', sufficient for RH.

Quote, Theorem 1(i), printed p2: 'is the reciprocal of a function'.

The theorem characterizes an integrable PF function by its bilateral Laplace
transform 1/Psi, where Psi is entire of Laguerre--Polya product form.
For r the reciprocal is exactly

    Psi_r(s)=product_(n>=1)(1+s/(pi*n^2))^2
            =[sinh(sqrt(pi*s))/sqrt(pi*s)]^2.

The last notation denotes its entire power series; there is no square-root
branch singularity. Its zeros are the negative numbers -pi*n^2, each double.
This verifies the additive source structure already obtained in section 3.
It does not locate xi zeros: Psi_r is not xi.

The original xi instead arises from the Mellin transform

    M_r(z)=integral_0^infinity t^(z-1)r(t)dt=2 xi(2z-2),
    F(w)=M_r(5/4-i*w/2)/(2A)=xi(1/2-i*w)/A.

The full reciprocal tails justify every complex z; continuation from the
initial half-plane is valid. Thus the relevant source question is zeros of
M_r on Re z=5/4, not zeros of Psi_r on the negative real line.
Schoenberg's Theorems 3--4 give an RH-equivalent PF condition for a DIFFERENT
object: Lambda(x)=(1/(2pi)) integral_R exp(-i*x*tau)/xi(1/2+tau) dtau.
Neither r nor f is that Lambda. Evenness removes harmless Fourier orientation
choices. No verified construction transfers r's PF property to Lambda.
The theorem is a verified sibling, but the application to our proved source

### 4.1. Analytic obstruction to simply demanding PF infinity of f

This is a direct application of Theorem 1 and its product, not a numerical
minor or an assumed RH zero. Let q=Phi/Z, Z=integral_R Phi>0; multiplication
by the positive normalization A/Z preserves every minor sign of f.
The full-source tail bounds imply

    M_q(s)=integral_R q(x)exp(-s*x)dx

is entire. All polynomial-weighted exponential moments converge uniformly
on compact s-sets. Suppose q were PF infinity. Schoenberg supplies an entire
Psi with M_q(s)Psi(s)=1 on a strip, hence everywhere by the identity theorem.
Thus Psi has no zeros. Its Laguerre--Polya product then reduces to
Psi(s)=C exp(-gamma*s^2+delta*s), gamma>=0. Since M_q(0)=1, C=1.
Evenness forces delta=0. Differentiating at zero gives
2gamma=integral x^2 q(x)dx>0, so gamma>0.

It follows that M_q(i*t)=exp(-gamma*t^2). Fourier uniqueness gives
q(x)=(4pi*gamma)^(-1/2)exp(-x^2/(4gamma)). This contradicts the full theta
bound q(x)<=C0 exp(-c exp(2|x|)) for large |x|: no positive Gaussian obeys
that bound. Consequently f is NOT PF infinity.

The coordinate change exposes the exact operation: for increasing real x_i,y_j,
put u_i=exp(2x_i), v_j=exp(2y_j). Then

    det[f(x_i-y_j)] = A^(-m) exp((5/2)(sum x_i-sum y_j))
                                      det[r(u_i/v_j)].

The proved source minors involve r(u_i-v_j); this changed table involves
r(u_i/v_j). The multiplier is positive. Thus full TN of this ratio kernel
is also excluded, while full TN of the difference kernel remains proved.

This proves only that the particular additive-minor property of r does not
survive as the same additive-minor property of f. It does not give a negative
V row, does not identify a finite minor order, and is not a refutation of RH.
It does not prohibit other useful properties surviving log/tilt. We do not
use the shelf's numerical PF5 report to establish this obstruction.
