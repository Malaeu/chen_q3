## Source benchmark and an explicit missing inequality

The source row norm (8111–8121) gives ||B^J||_2²<<Z^(M'+epsilon). The full quantitative Gram bound (8340–8364), with P_a=Y'^2/Q~Z^(1/8), gives ||A||_2²<<(1+|v|)^C (Q/Y')P_a^(1/6) Z^epsilon in this geometry. Cauchy therefore gives

|S_J(v)| <<(1+|v|)^C Z^epsilon
          (Q/Y')^(1/2)P_a^(1/12) Z^(M'/2).                 (1)

Seek instead the SAME sum with an additional factor Z^(-sigma(d)), uniformly over its actual fixed-data parameters, rescaled tuples and v (with an integrable polynomial seminorm loss). No moving coefficients may be removed to get that factor. A uniform gain in(1) is sufficient, not necessary: cancellation between subsets or tuples could be another route.

The outside tuple count Z^d, weight Z^(-3d/2), and X'^(1/2) cost Z^(-d) in total relative to d=0. Consequently such a pairing gain would give the full physical exponent

L <= 3/16 - min_J[d+sigma(d)].                            (2)

At the fixed scalar C(s)=s-11/16, an improvement to the low threshold beta0 requires min_J[d+sigma(d)]>=7/8-beta0, with appropriate epsilon margins. Approaching beta0=1/2 therefore needs gains approaching3/8. High-side continuation/nonvanishing for this SAME probe would still have to be proved; (2) alone is not RH.

As an arithmetic benchmark only, hypothetical square-root cancellation across Q row terms corresponds to sigma(d)=M'/2=5/12-d. It would give L=3/16-5/12=-11/48, which is1/24 below C(1/2)=-3/16. No independence or random-sign hypothesis is asserted, and no such square-root bound has been established. This computation specifies the required scale rather than supplying evidence for it.

For a small gain delta>0, only subsets with d<delta need a new factor beyond the source absolute bound. If delta is smaller than every positive slot length, only J=empty is costly on the low side. Slot choices and the same-probe high argument must be checked before using that simplification. For the RH-sized gain3/8, every d<=1/6 still needs improvement; discarding the rescaled subsets would be invalid.

## Full-pairing good-prime resonance is rapidly negligible

Proof of the support assertion: at a prime put k=v_p(s), e=v_p(c) in{0,1}, l=v_p(n), a=e+3l. If k=0 and a>0, the local character mean survives only if a=0 mod6, forcing e=0 and l even. If k>0 and a=0, the Gauss mean is zero by additive orthogonality. If k>=2 and a>0, the character restricts m to units, where the prime-power Gauss factor vanishes pointwise. If k=1 and a>0, the remaining unit character has exponent a-1, so survival requires e=1 and l even. These statements include principal unit masks at exponents divisible by6; no exceptional prime-power term survives. Global Gauss scaling supplies the displayed surviving function without guessing local CRT phases. Source anchors:642,1043,3881–3893.
