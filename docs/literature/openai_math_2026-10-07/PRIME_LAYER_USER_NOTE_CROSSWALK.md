## Algebra

For n=p^j m with p not dividing m, the difference W(p^(j+1)m)-W(p^j m) is exactly sum_(r|m,p^(j+1)r<=R)b_(p^(j+1)r). Multiplicativity of c and its prime-power formula make it p^(j+1) sum_(p not dividing r)(a_(p^(j+1)r)-a_(p^(j+2)r))c_r(m), with a zero outside the finite support. No endpoint is discarded.

Expansion of |H+B|² gives the exact mixed term plus |B|². Counting common multiples yields the floor(X/lcm(d,e)) Gram matrix on integers. A new allowed prime uses this formula only when old b coefficients remain fixed and p is absent from old r,s. Conditional-mean preservation implies nondecrease of the normalized second moment on a common probability space; it is not a sign theorem for arbitrary finite-window transitions. These are identities, not quantitative cancellation suppliers.
