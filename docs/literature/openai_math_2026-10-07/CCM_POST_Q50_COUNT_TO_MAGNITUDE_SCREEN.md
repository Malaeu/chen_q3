positive and one negative eigenvalue, independently of t. Its minimum is

## Follow-up: exact signed second moment and its actual fallback price

Let d=dim ran P=2M-2 eventually and Z_rho=mult(rho)m^(rho-1/2)S_P(rho-1/2).

Without splitting any crossed residues,

 tr(Rsign^2) = (1/2) tr(Z* Z) + (1/2) Re tr(Z^2).

Both right-hand quantities include every rho,sigma cross term. Dropping
Re tr(Z^2) or replacing the terms by absolute products changes the price.
The identity alone does not bound this combined moment.

For the presently available bound, the inherited full physical prime norm
is ||A||<=12sqrt(m). Equation(5) of the original-P contour note gives

Since B=O_epsilon(m^epsilon L log L), e=o(1), this spends exponent
1/2+epsilon/2. It is worse than keeping the operator norm fallback directly;
we discard this completion and retain the old upper. This is an upper
certificate price, NOT a lower bound for the actual joint second moment.

Rminus=max(-Rsign,0) is the spectral negative part of the COMPLETE Rsign.
