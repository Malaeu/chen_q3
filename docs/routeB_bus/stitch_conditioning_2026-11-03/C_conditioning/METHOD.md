The carrier is the original even literal Fourier CCM space, dimension m+1, N=m, L=log m, with zero extension outside [-L/2,L/2]. It is not the step-profile ambient space used by the separate stitching experiment. The primary schedule is κ=1+(1/4)(1+log L)/L; secondary c=1/2; control κ=1. All cutoff comparisons are strict γ<T. m≤120 has L<16 and is outside the asymptotic regime of the shrinking-κ theorem.

Raw physical Fourier evaluation rows use the exact rational formula in capacity.py. A critical pair contributes one raw row; an off-critical quartet contributes raw Re and Im rows, without sqrt(2). The corresponding Krein weights are 2ν and diag(4ν,−4ν). Multiplicity weights the form and does not add derivative constraints. DH includes all four off-line quartets in the supplied census below230 (85.699,114.163,166.479,176.702), not merely the first. Plant adds the prescribed quartet 2π/log2+i/4 to ζ. The structural rank is min(q,m+1), after distinct-orbit realification. Tall E is retained; J=0 is explicitly flagged. No fictitious positive quiet dimension is inserted. For J nonzero, twice-reorthogonalized MP80 row MGS is performed once at the largest required prefix and reused for all cutoffs. MP orthogonality and row-rank residuals are guarded. The original implementation converted the MP 0/1 projector to float and diagonalized it. The faster implementation converts the already-orthonormal MP row frame and uses complete float QR; comparison with MP projectors in qr_projector_validation.json gives operator differences ≤1.67e−15 and kernel residuals ≤1.09e−15. No inverse of a poorly conditioned float raw Gram matrix is used to build J.

For raw σmin(F)=σmin(E) (nonzero minimum, F=E U), well-resolved float singular vectors receive an MP80 Rayleigh and residual replay; this branch is permitted only when the float singular value exceeds100 times a conservative float radius. A bounded small-cell full MP SVD handles slow convergence. Unresolved weak large cases remain explicitly unresolved. Early pilot rows retain full MP80 SVD or inverse-iteration labels. Selected full MP120 SVD validations are in validation_120.json; raw tiny singular values are never inferred from float roundoff. These are numerical diagnostics, not interval enclosures.

The source matrices and sorted spectra are supplied by matrices/. Matrix float radii are diagnostic and were calibrated against selected higher-order assembly/source MP matrices. A cached MP eigenvalue is used for λ_r when available; otherwise a float value is used only above its numerical radius. Tiny uncached λ_r is UNRESOLVED_TINY_FLOAT, and the ratio 2σ²/λ_r is left blank. The sorted index r is zero-based: it is the first index above the dimension-r quiet sector.

Full D=QᵀAQ, B=UᵀAQ, G=UᵀAU are retained. δ=||AQ|| is the full operator residual, not ||D||. At C=2, S=G−2I−B(D−2I)⁻¹Bᵀ is formed only when D−2I is numerically separated from zero. Conservative threshold-sign guards label unresolved cases rather than assigning a sign. No claim is made at a singular eliminated block through an unqualified pseudoinverse.

EDGE_ZERO means |T−γ|<π/log(γ/(2π)), exactly half the local mean spacing. Every flagged cell is repeated at T±that same halfspacing, using the same A. Included-gap statistics use distinct included heights; Re/Im rows of a quartet are not a zero-spacing pair. The last-included to first-excluded lookahead gap is separate. Requested Lehmer flag is gL/(2π)<0.3. Adaptive κ sectors are derived from the same A spectrum at µ=m⁻²,m⁻⁴, with signed counts including ground, and no new eigenproblem. At a sector's lower zero height the boundary is excluded (strict γ<T); its upper zero height is included as a valid T endpoint.
