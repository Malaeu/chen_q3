paper's definitions alone: `\hatΦ = Ξ` (to `10^{-15}` at `z=1,2,5`), `∫Φ = ξ(½) = 0.4971207782`,
`A = 0.565466013092`, `∫f_0 = 0.8791346724`, evenness of `Φ` (to `2·10^{-14}`).
**`Q(f_0) = 1.0·10^{-15}`** with `c_A = γ+log 8π+π/2 = 5.3721834192` — so the normalisation, the
sign of the pole term `2A_+\overline{A_-} = 0.5/A²`, and the sign and factor 2 of the prime term in (1)
are mutually consistent and match Weil's formula. **`B(f_0,v) = −7.5·10^{-16}`** for a genuine

## Recommendation

**Major revision.** The paper's positive core is genuine and, where it can be checked, correct: the
CCM normalisation of `Φ`, the identity `\hatΦ=Ξ`, the radical property of `f_0`, and — the real
contribution — the ground-state representation of Theorem 4.1 with the closed-form density
`b(t)=a(t)−2cosh(t/2)` and its sign change at the plastic number. I verified that identity numerically
to `10^{-12}` and re-derived it by hand; it is right, it is clean, and it is the reason the paper should
not be rejected. Theorem 5.3 and Lemma 5.2 are also correct. But the paper is not publishable in its
present state, for three reasons. **First**, the two auxiliary impossibility results that the abstract,
the introduction and the ledger all advertise (Props. 5.7 and 5.8) are not proved, and their stated
arguments are not repairable as written: 5.7 measures a radical-approximating sequence in the full norm
instead of the quotient norm, and 5.8 applies `|T|` to a distribution and then draws a conclusion about
the negative part of `ν` from a divergence of its positive part. Two of the four ledger items therefore
have no theorem behind them. **Second**, the single step on which every other result depends — the
validity of the explicit formula (2) outside `C_c^∞`, needed for Prop. 3.3 — is never justified, and the
alternative argument the paper offers in parentheses is a genuine logical error (null vector ≠ radical
vector for an indefinite form). The statement happens to be true, as my numerics show, but a paper whose
entire architecture rests on one proposition cannot leave that proposition to a numerical check.
**Third**, the surrounding claims exceed the delivery: the Lean formalisation is described in §8 in terms
that App. B contradicts, "an exact second-jet formula" rests on an underivable expansion plus a
regression I could not reproduce from the printed data, and a LaTeX misconfiguration presents every
Proposition, Corollary and Example — including the two unproved propositions — to the reader as
"Theorem". Fix findings 1–7 and the paper becomes a solid, honestly negative contribution; leave them
and the referee cannot tell which of its statements are theorems.
