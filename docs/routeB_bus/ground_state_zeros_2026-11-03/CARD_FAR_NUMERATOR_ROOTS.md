# Far numerator roots and boundary curvature

HYPOTHESIS: the zeta ground has a numerically faithful source-zero sector and a separate far-real numerator-root tail; the negative controls concentrate almost all their positive numerator roots near the carrier frequency range. This is a finite geometric discriminator, not a source-sign theorem.

Pilot evidence at m300: the even zeta numerator has183 roots below T=370.179625... and117 positive roots above it, the largest at61162.0517... (165.22T). The DH and planted even numerators have299 positive roots below T plus one pure-imaginary Fourier root; their largest positive roots are330.777 and331.051. Their winning odd-ground numerators have299 positive roots and no comparable far-real tail. Other m and parity changes must remain visible in the full grid.

There is a direct finite algebra check. At b=L/2, using derivatives from the interior of the window, the even numerator roots z_j satisfy

    sum_j z_j^2 = sum_{n=1}^m omega_n^2 + f''(b)/f(b).

For an odd numerator replace the boundary quotient by f'''(b)/f'(b). The sums run over one z_j per numerator root in z^2, including a negative contribution from a pure-imaginary root. They are not sums of absolute squares, and the derivatives are not distributional derivatives of the zero extension. This is the standard Vieta coefficient identity for the finite rational Fourier formula. Both true and fake worlds obey it; it supplies no arithmetic inequality.

The m300 even boundary quotients are about4.5300e9 for zeta, -3.7599e5 for DH and1.0654e4 for the plant. All36 selected world/parity/window Vieta replays agree to relative error below4.45e-180. This explains how a far-real root tail can be encoded in extremely small boundary values and curvature; it does not derive its cofinal behavior.

What would kill or limit the candidate: a converged zeta cell with no comparable source-zero sector/tail, a matched negative control with the same recovery and tail geometry, or sensitivity of the far roots to source precision. Pole/origin cancellation, leading-coefficient floors and pure-imaginary roots must be retained.

Hand off: determine whether the source controls the boundary quotient and the location of the free numerator roots. A universal Vieta identity alone cannot distinguish Euler arithmetic or close D0.
