# Source, solver and reproduction

The source is the literal even L2-orthonormal cosine carrier, dimension m+1,N=m,L=logm. source/split.py builds C and B separately at400-bit MPFR with GL192×8panels. C contains the full archimedean constant, arch integral, tail and original even pole; B retains each negative-signed arithmetic correlation. DH has its own odd gamma factor, no pole and signed b_D(n), with non-prime-powers retained. double2 modifies only integer2 before multiplying B by u; powers4,8,… are not doubled. The supplied verdict is read as a control definition, not as proof of positivity or a new route instruction.

All855 splits were computed. C+B and frozen A1 agree within3.56e−15; original source regressions at80,120,248,300 are exact after float conversion. The16 check is explicitly a fresh original-builder comparison. Sign-group conservation is checked in MP to5.27e−119. GL192/256 pilot differences are<=1.16e−115. None is an interval quadrature bound. All endpoint algebra uses the stored C and B pencil consistently; the tiny C+B−storedA1 rounding difference stays in the numerical guard.

Primary ρ_m=1000 eps64(m+1) max_world(1,||C||₂+||B||₂), ε_m=100ρ_m. The same value is used inζ,double2,DH for eachm. Additional factors10 and1000 are run at16,24,40,80,120,200,248,300. Error scale varies with u as e_C+|u|e_B; ρ max(1,|u|) is the stated heuristic budget, not an outward norm enclosure. Common ε grows withm, so raw full-grid width growth must not be interpreted as intrinsic I0 growth. COUPLING plots offsets and offsets/ε separately; MP sweeps demonstrate the difference.

All real u are allowed. If G(a)=C+aB+εI is strictly positive, Cholesky and H=L^-1BL^-T give exact affine PSD endpoint formulas: lower a−1/hmax if hmax>0, otherwise−infinity; upper a−1/hmin if hmin<0, otherwise+infinity. The physical boundary eigenvector is normalized L^-T v, not v. Its slope is xᵀBx; it is positive on the lower side and negative on the upper side, and du/dε=−1/(xᵀBx) for a simple boundary mode. Whitened modes are the first-exit modes of the pencil, not generally eigenvectors of A1 or an arbitrarily chosen quiet compression basis.

When u1 is not a strict anchor, minimum-eigenvector and B-extreme Rayleigh constraints α_i+uβ_i>=0 enclose any feasible interval. Midpoint cuts shrink this enclosure. Incompatible lower/upper cuts yield a two-vector convex dual combination with slope≈0 and negative constant. Its residual slope and guard margin are saved. These are numerical point-pencil diagnostics, not exact rational dual certificates for the analytic source. Degenerate/tangent/recession cases are explicitly unresolved; the implementation does not infer emptiness from a missed grid or from QZ roots. All actual primary cases are bounded nonempty or guarded empty. Endpoint inside tests are positive and outside Rayleigh tests negative. Boundary residual and independent eigensolver checks accompany the table.

J uses κ1,T=2πm/logm, strictγ<T, one real row per critical pair and two per off-line quartet. Complete even realification is performed before row/null-space orthogonalization at768bits. Source nodes are the preserved ζ601 and DH900 tables from the preceding diagnostic work. There is no SVD numerical-rank truncation. Quiet NPZ intermediates are regenerated rather than transferred. double2 reuses Jζ ONLY as a reference; it does not purport to use zeros of the altered source. Projection mass is ||Qᵀx||², with small above1 roundoff retained in raw receipts. Individual quiet modes below the float guard are labeled a cluster rather than assigned invariant ordinal meaning.

MP pilots build C/B at400bits and GL256, then use mpmath120digits, true MP quiet frames and dense small-matrix eigensolvers. Their empirical source/span floor is about1e−114; all three reported quiet minima exceed it. ε1e−100 plateau values are not rounded by adding them to1 in binary64. mp_pilots/RESULT.md and CHECKS.json record source, orthogonality, endpoint and Cholesky residuals. Only16,24,40 were escalated; no full-range intrinsic minimum or I0 is claimed. No assumption of sqrt(ε) behavior is made: the scaling depends on the nullspace slope and off-block adaptation, as the synthetic review tests illustrate.
