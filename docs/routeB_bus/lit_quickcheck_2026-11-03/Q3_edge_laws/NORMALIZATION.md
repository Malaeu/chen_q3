External s=1 formQ_L equals our physical Weil formW. Both use ordinary
Fourier exp(ixt), physical L2 normalization and the complete prime powers.
The half-line spectral factor1/pi equals full-line1/(2pi) on the real tests
in the paper. The physical cross term2F(i/2)F(-i/2) retains both poles.
Their support[-L,L] has our full lengthell=2L; hence m=exp(2L) and
d lambda/dell=(1/2)d lambda/dL. No eigenvalue multiplier is required.

This does not identify their renormalized edge traceA with our finite
Fourier endpoint value. Neither the tail scale nor the source of a kink
can be crosswalked by only changing a constant. See EXTRACTION.md.
