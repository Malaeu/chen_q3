# Separate correction to the immutable brief

At a prime power n, Ψ is continuous and its derivative jumps **down** by −Λ(n)/√n. Such a hinge can be a local maximum; it does not supply the prescribed local minima. For x=e^t≥2, between events,

Ψ′(t)=c/2+atanh(x^(−1/2))+atan(x^(−1/2))+2(√x−1/√x)−Σ_{n≤x}Λ(n)/√n,

Ψ″(t)=√x−1/[√x(x²−1)]>0.

Consequently a negative-to-positive derivative crossing inside an event interval is its unique minimum. The scan tests these roots and interval endpoints. The initial interval 1<x<2 is split at the positive root of x³−x−1, the only sign change of the smooth second derivative, and stationary roots are checked separately.

Because Ψ(0)=0 and Ψ is continuous there, strict positivity for t>0 does not imply a strictly positive uniform lower bound on (0,T]. The report distinguishes a nonzero candidate minimum from this origin infimum. No edit was made to the published brief.
