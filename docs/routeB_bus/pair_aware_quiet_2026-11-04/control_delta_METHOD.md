literal physical source with 400-bit MPFR, GL192 on eight panels. This is the
mid-extension's pinned recipe, not the high-extension GL256 recipe. All signed
DH integer atoms, the correct odd gamma/no-pole normalization, and the plant's
original rank-two signed quartet contribution remain in the source. The
regeneration authorization applies only to local dense arrays; outputs carry
array and file hashes.

strict inclusion γ<Ω. A quartet inserts its real and imaginary constraints
atomically. The original mid-extension ζ and DH600 numerical zero censuses
remain unchanged. Critical rows omit their nonzero common physical scalar for

the complex physical scalar. The original native 768-bit twice-reorthogonalized

quantity is the full residual δ=||AQ||₂, not ||QᵀAQ|| or λ on a best-gap sector.
