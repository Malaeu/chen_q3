# Novelty check 2026-11-06: exact finite Ramanujan expansion + Selberg symmetry + positive form, for a sign

Status: Mythos literature check, limited: 6 web queries plus 1 Consensus query. CHALLENGER / NOT_RH.

## Found separately (FACT)

1. **Finite Ramanujan expansion of the truncated Λ_N.**
   - Coppola–Murty–Saha, JNT 174 (2017), arXiv:1612.03136; Coppola–Murty II, arXiv:1705.07193.
   - Laporta, Abh. Math. Sem. Hamburg (2024), arXiv:2204.01581.
   - Use so far: shifted correlations / twin primes. Laporta proves an inequality EQUIVALENT to the Hardy–Littlewood twin-prime formula, not the formula itself.
2. **Selberg symmetry formula.**
   - The error is O(x) elementarily.
   - Under RH it is O(√x·log³x) (Saidak, Math. Scand. 103 (2008)).
   - Elementary PNT error terms: Diamond–Steinig (Invent. Math. 1970), Lavrik–Sobirov, Lu. None has a power saving.
3. **Nyman–Beurling Gram matrices and their quadratic-form decompositions.**
   - Ehm, arXiv:2405.06349 (2024): a Gram-matrix decomposition with reciprocity series S(x) = Σ R(nx).
   - Bettin–Conrey 2013; Maier–Rassias 2017/2018 (constants inconsistent); Alouges–Darses–Hillion JTNB 2022.
4. **Truncated divisor sums Λ_R.** Goldston–Yıldırım (PLMS) give correlations; under GRH, Ω± results for the variation of the PNT error.

## Warning (FACT)

Laporta, arXiv:2006.04547 (2020, preprint, unrefereed), claimed an unconditional proof of the Hardy–Littlewood twin-prime and binary Goldbach conjectures via a finite Ramanujan expansion. The later refereed paper (arXiv:2204.01581) proves only an inequality equivalent to the HL formula.

Lesson: the finite Ramanujan expansion is exact, and that exactness makes it easy to hide the whole difficulty in an "error" term. Every claimed sign must be checked against this trap.
