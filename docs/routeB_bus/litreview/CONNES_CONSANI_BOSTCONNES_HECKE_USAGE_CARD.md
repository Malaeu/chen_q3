## Single most promising unconditional piece
**Connes–Consani 2006.13771, Theorem 4.7 (p.27, eq. 83):**
  "Let S be the orthogonal projection of L²(R)_ev on the closed subspace S(1,1). The following functional is positive:
   `Tr(ϑ(f) S) = W_∞(f) + ∫ f(ρ^{-1}) ε(ρ) d*ρ, ∀f ∈ C_c^∞(R*₊)`, where ε(ρ^{-1}) = ε(ρ) and, for ρ ≥ 1,
   `ε(ρ) = Σ_n λ(n)/√(1−λ(n)²) · ⟨ξ_n | ϑ(ρ^{-1}) ζ_n⟩`."
Why: an EXACT identity (our §2: only identities reach a critical constant), unconditional, valid for ALL test functions
with no support restriction, writing the archimedean part of the Weil form as a manifestly nonnegative Hilbert–Schmidt
trace minus a remainder explicitly computable from prolate data. AS IT STANDS it fails §4.2's radical clause and §4.5's

Connes announces it as "makes sense in the general semi-local case", not as done.
