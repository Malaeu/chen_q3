A Loewner divided-difference formula for the prime contribution
  in the localized Weil quadratic form, and a parity sign law
                                           Breno Andrade
                                             ArqueOps
                                       breno@arqueops.com.br

                                           June 15, 2026


                                              Abstract
         In the localized/truncated Weil quadratic form of Connes–Consani–Moscovici
     (arXiv:2511.22755) and Connes–van Suijlekom (arXiv:2511.23257), the prime contribution
     enters the Galerkin matrix as a divided difference (Loewner matrix) of an explicit oscillatory
     symbol. We record two consequences. First (Proposition 1), for any test vector v the prime
     quadratic form admits an exact closed Loewner representation in terms of the Fourier symbol
     Wv and the arithmetic frequencies ωq = 1 − log q/ log c. The identity is exact; we validate it
     numerically at c = 5, 13, 53 to working precision. Second, parity of the symbol fixes the sign
     structure: for the even vector C = cosh(x/2) the symbol WC is real and positive, and we
     prove (Lemma 3, with an explicit Galerkin-truncation bound) that the prime contribution
     in C is non-positive term by term (strict for q < c) — the primes cooperate. For the odd
     vector S = sinh(x/2) the symbol WS is purely imaginary and sign-changing, so the per-prime
     contributions have mixed sign and the positivity of ⟨S, P S⟩ holds only as a sum in which
     the large primes outweigh the small ones — the primes compete. We make no claim about
     even-simplicity or the Riemann Hypothesis; rather, the odd sector is reduced to one explicit
     term-by-term competition inequality, tied to the odd-sector positivity problem.

5    Conclusion
Parity of the test vector controls the sign mechanism of the prime piece through its Fourier
symbol:

                                                    3
  sector                 symbol                        prime contribution
  even (C = cosh x2 )    WC real, > 0                  non-positive term by term; strict in total
                                                       (cooperation)
  odd (S = sinh x2 )     WS imaginary, oscillating     mixed sign; positive only as a sum (com-
                                                       petition)

The Loewner representation (4) turns the prime piece of the localized Weil form into an explicit
object in the arithmetic frequencies ωq = 1 − log q/ log c; the even sign law (Corollary 4) is
elementary and unconditional, while the odd sector is reduced to the single term-by-term
competition inequality (9). This inequality is the prime-side form of the odd-sector positivity
problem in this model. In the continuum Weil setting it is RH-adjacent in the Yoshida sense. We
make no claim toward it here. The value of the present note is the explicit, reusable representation
and the clean separation of the two sectors into cooperation versus arithmetic competition.
