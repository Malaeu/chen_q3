                                                  THE SCREW LINE OF THE RIEMANN ZETA-FUNCTION
                                                              AND ITS APPLICATIONS

                                                                                 MASATOSHI SUZUKI


                                                  Abstract. We investigate the screw line corresponding to the screw function associ-
                                                  ated with the Riemann zeta-function under the Riemann hypothesis and derive three
                                                  necessary and sufficient conditions for the Riemann hypothesis as applications. One
                                                  of them explains the non-negativity of the Weil distribution by means of the norm.
