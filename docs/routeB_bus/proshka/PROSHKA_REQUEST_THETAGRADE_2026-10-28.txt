3. Q1 - BOTH OPEN LOAD-BEARING ROPES ARE THETA-GRADED, GIVEN G1 (verification)

Claim Q1a (observer, not checked).  Assume on every late cell: G1 (simple
bottom eigenspace with eta . xi != 0), hence G2 real zeros by CCM Thm 5.10 /
the Lean theorem above; G4; G3c; hmode/hchi; (T5).  Suppose zeta has a zero
with Re = 1/2 + delta', delta' > 0.  Then F_m cannot converge to Xi uniformly
on any small disc around the centered zero z0 (Hurwitz, F_m real-rooted).
Since T_m -> Xi there, sup_K |F_m - T_m| stays >= c > 0 for all large m, for
every compact K containing that disc.  By (T3), for every H > delta',
  alpha_m >= c' m^(-H/2) (log m)^(-1/2) - 4 t_m  for all large m.
So no rate alpha_m = O(m^(-a)) with a > delta'/2 holds.

Claim Q1b (corollary).  Under the same hypotheses, ANY proved rate
alpha_m = O(m^(-a) (log m)^A) with a > 0 gives a zero-free strip
1/2 < Re s < 1/2 + 2a.  With ZF78 (Theta <= 7/8), a > 3/16 gives RH.
So every positive tracking rate is a quasi-RH strip next to the line.

Claim Q1c.  The SP floor exponent is exactly Theta - 1/2 from both sides for
an arbitrary zero-free abscissa Theta (upper side: answer3 contour shift;
lower side: NEXT.md:60-61).  Answer3 was proved for the MTY region; the
observer generalized it without a check.

4. Q2 - IS G1 ITSELF THETA-GRADED? (the main question)

Object: the literal carrier of PAPER_CHAIN.md G1 block: K_j =
sourceCCMFiniteMatrix i_j, q_j = selected Ferrers row, a_j = Re(q_j* K_j q_j),
r_j = (K_j - a_j) q_j.  G1 needs, on every late cell: simple bottom
eigenspace; eta . xi != 0 (even ground state); cellwise gap delta_j > 0 on the
complement; ||r_j|| / delta_j -> 0; odd-sector floor (hoddEv).

Known: K1 kills a constant floor with no zero input.  So part of G1 already
fails for a structural reason, not because of Theta.

Observer's candidate witness (probability 0.35): "parity twin".  An off-line
zero pair at +-z0 makes both the even and the odd sector reach
lambda <= -c m^delta (NEXT.md:60-61 witness, symmetrized).  If both sector
minima carry the same leading term, then the even/odd gap closes relative to
||r_j||, or the ground state flips to odd infinitely often.  Then G1 fails
whenever RH fails, and G1 is Theta-graded.

Task: decide one of three.
  (A) THETA_WITNESS: an off-critical zero forces, on infinitely many late
      cells, one of: a degenerate bottom eigenspace, eta . xi = 0, or
      ||r_j|| / delta_j not -> 0.  Give the mechanism with exact orders.
  (B) NOT_GRADED: a named obstruction to every such witness, for example a
      mechanism that keeps the ground state simple and even for any Theta.
      Say which part of G1 then remains provable without zero input.
  (C) UNDECIDED: the first exact fact needed to decide, with objects and orders.

treat the question as RH.
