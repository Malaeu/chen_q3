## ROUTE MAP

### 1. Прямой ответ

Обитатель `Satz9SourceData` **не обязан быть вычислительно построен**.
Существования достаточно. В классической математике и в `noncomputable` Lean
из

```text
exists S, Satz9SourceData lambda theta S
```

можно выбрать приватный witness через устранение существования или
`Classical.choose`. Это не ослабляет theorem и не требует формулы для
`ps_n^0`. `[ABSTRACT][LEAN]`

Но три утверждения нельзя смешивать:

| Уровень | Что даёт ссылка на источник | Статус |
|---|---|---|
| Бумажный | Теорема существования и свойства `ps_n^0` могут быть приняты из Meixner--Schäfke/DLMF | `[COFINAL_FAMILY][PAPER]` |
| Условный Lean | Теорема может принять это существование явной гипотезой и выбрать witness внутри proof | `[COFINAL_FAMILY][LEAN]` |
| Безусловный Lean | Kernel должен получить proof-term существования; prose-card его не создаёт | `[COFINAL_FAMILY][CONDITIONAL]` |

Именно поэтому ответ не «строить обязательно», но и не «usage card уже закрыл
Lean-floor».

### 2. Почему Satz 9 и существование относятся к одному классу

В математическом proof ledger различия нет. Satz 9 — внешний theorem о
first-kind mode; существование этого mode, его source eigenvalue, regularity,
parity and nonzero even centre — другой внешний theorem bundle. Оба можно
цитировать, если exact source location and normalization are verified.
`[ABSTRACT][PAPER]`

DLMF Chapter 30 confirms the relevant classical shape independently:

```text
Ps_n^m is the first-kind eigenfunction belonging to lambda_n^m(gamma^2);
these bounded/regular solutions exist at the discrete source eigenvalues;
for n-m even the unnormalised ODE solution may be fixed by p(0)=1, p'(0)=0.
```

Thus there is no mathematical requirement to implement the special function
from a series. The source existence can be imported as a theorem. The current

### 5. The exact legal existential supplier

The admissible external theorem is not merely

```text
Nonempty (Satz9SourceData lambda_k theta_project).
```

That statement is too weak: it hides the eigenvalue crosswalk and can be
inhabited by the project mode.

The source theorem must first quantify an independently named source value:

```text
for k and j in {0,2},
there exist theta_src and a first-kind source mode p_{j,k}
such that
  degree = 2*j;
  order = 0;
  paper parameter = gamma_k^2;
  paper eigenvalue = theta_src;
  the physical rescaling of p_{j,k} supplies
    Satz9SourceData(lambda_k, theta_src);
  the normalized Satz-9 estimate is about this same p_{j,k}.
```

Only afterwards may W13.7 prove

```text
theta_src = theta_project
```

and transport the package to the receiver's expected type. This keeps the
source mode, the source eigenvalue and the rate on one object. It also prevents
a source/rate witness mismatch. `[COFINAL_FAMILY][CONDITIONAL]`

### 6. Does the floor collapse to citation plus W13.7?

**At paper level: yes, with one guard.** The citation must be exact enough to
supply the complete source theorem above. Then the only substantive
source/project identity is W13.7. `[COFINAL_FAMILY][PAPER]`

**At kernel level: not yet.** If the citation is retained as an explicit theorem
hypothesis, the downstream Lean assembly is clean and uses the standard axiom

unconditional Lean theorem is demanded, the existence theorem itself must be
formalized. A new global project `axiom` would change the axiom profile and is
not authorized. `[COFINAL_FAMILY][LEAN]`

There is one more bookkeeping guard. `Satz9SourceData` lives in physical
coordinates. If the cited theorem is stated only for dimensionless

```text

```

and the resulting physical flux equation must be proved or included explicitly
in the paper supplier. That is W13.8/9. It is cheap assembly, not a second
analytic wall, but it cannot disappear silently inside the word “exists.”
