0. WHAT WE FOUND (DIAGNOSTIC_NEVER_A_PROOF; literal K_m, modes -m..m, N = m, L = log m)

(a) Radical family. h of MARKOV (4.3) has h-hat(z) = 4 xi(1/2 - iz); every derivative h^(k)
    has transform (iz)^k 4 xi(1/2 - iz), so h, h', h'', ... are exact null vectors of the
    global Weil form (k(h^(k), .) = 0). Any translate of them is null too (factor e^{-i delta z}).
(b) The window bottoms lie in this family. Least squares of the sector bottoms on a window
    grid (pingeom_fit.py, dps 100): relative residual 2e-6 .. 2.4e-4 at m = 6..24 in
    span{h, h'', h''''} (even) and span{h', h''', h^(5)} (odd). Weights (c_i ||D_i||) reach a
    plateau: even (0.99857, 0.05327, 0.00359) at m = 24, (0.99858, 0.05322, 0.00345) at m = 20;
    odd (0.99380, 0.11081, 0.00904) at m = 24, (0.99383, 0.11056, 0.00870) at m = 20.
    So the bottoms do NOT converge to h, h' but to a fixed mixture.
(c) One heat time for both sectors. Raw ratio s = c_1/c_0: even 0.003082 (m=24), 0.003079 (20);
    odd 0.003007 (24), 0.003001 (20). First order f = h + s h'' = Fourier 4 xi (1 - s z^2), i.e.
    h run through the heat equation for time s. Second order c_2/(s^2/2) = 0.75 .. 0.81
    (heatsmooth_check.py), so exp(s d^2/dx^2) h is a first-order match only.
    Selection by concentration is refuted: minimisers of int w f^2 / int f^2 in the same spans
    (w = x^2, x^4, cosh x, e^{2|x|}) give the h'' weight about -0.19 (even), the window +0.053.
    The bottom is broader than h (pingeom_select.py).
(d) The odd bottom is the derivative of the even bottom (odd_is_derivative.py, model-free,
    both synthesized from K_m): 1 - cos(f_O, f_E') = 9.3e-5 (m=4), 2.6e-5 (6), 1.9e-5 (8),
    4.6e-6 (12), 1.5e-6 (16), monotone. Observer reading: the window breaks the translation
    symmetry of the radical family; the odd bottom is the translation mode of the even bottom.
(e) Two-sided pin (shake_probe.py, shake_fake_atom.py). Hellmann-Feynman speeds of the sector
    bottoms when an atom at shift a moves right: even speed > 0 for every a in (0, L), m = 4..16;

    0.574 (20), 0.577 (24). For pure h' the threshold (minimum of its autocorrelation,
    R(a) ~ int t^2 Xi(t)^2 cos(ta) dt) is 0.547636729746 (two independent quadratures agree to
    12 digits, pingeom_limit.py). All prime shifts log n >= log 2 = 0.693 lie beyond a*.
    The pin itself is shape, not arithmetic (a fake atom anywhere beyond a* is pinned the same way).
(f) Geometry breaks with the sign (break_sign_keep_shape.py, m = 12): shifting log 2 and its
    powers by eps gives lam0(K_E) = -6.7e-27, -1.2e-20, -7.2e-12 for eps = -1e-26, -1e-20, -1e-12,
    and the radical-family residual of the bottom grows 1.95e-6 -> 9.45e-4 -> 0.392.
    The clean xi-geometry exists only for the exact primes.
(g) No confinement law (confine_probe.py). lam0_E / (tail energy of the plateau vector beyond

    5.46e-38 (16), 2.58e-46 (20), 2.69e-54 (24). Heat-flow deformation e^{t x^2} h (de Bruijn-Newman)
