from the decisive ledger.

This repair does not help the route: the source-locked `n <= m` window part alone
already leaves a supercritical available envelope.

### 4. Two-mode Feshbach is not a new rate mechanism

The committed exact source-action split has the shape

\[
s_k(R_kx_k-a_kx_k)
=t_k\big((R_k-a_k)eE_k+(R_k-a_k)gE_k\big).
\]

If the retained space is

\[
U_k=\operatorname{span}\{eE_k,gE_k\},
\]

then the Feshbach off-diagonal block is generated by precisely the two vectors

\[
Q_{U_k}(R_k-a_k)eE_k,
\qquad
Q_{U_k}(R_k-a_k)gE_k.
\]

The first contains the retained-prime action already identified as open; the
second is the previously unsupplied target-action channel. Calling them a
coupling matrix does not supply their norm.

second retained direction leaves an arbitrarily large complement coupling and
an equally large Schur self-energy. Therefore R2 is killed **as an independent
bypass**. This does not kill Schur/Feshbach methods when a genuinely new
