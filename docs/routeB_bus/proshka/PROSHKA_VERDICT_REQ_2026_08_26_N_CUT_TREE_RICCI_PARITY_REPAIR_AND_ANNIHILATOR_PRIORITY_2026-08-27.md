## 2. The reported parity proof of Ricci frustration is false

For ordered nodes `n_i`, the literal off-diagonal Loewner matrix is

\[
K_{ij}=\frac{\beta_i-\beta_j}{n_i-n_j}.
\]

A diagonal sign switch `S=diag(s_i)`, `s_i in {+1,-1}`, does not change the
sign product around any cycle.  To switch all nonzero off-diagonal entries to
the negative Markov/Laplacian sign, every triangle must satisfy

\[
\operatorname{sgn}(K_{ij}K_{jk}K_{ki})=-1.
\]

The report correctly computes, for `i<j<k`,

\[
\operatorname{sgn}(K_{ij}K_{jk}K_{ki})
=
\operatorname{sgn}
\bigl((\beta_j-\beta_i)(\beta_k-\beta_j)(\beta_k-\beta_i)\bigr).
\]

The invalid step is:

```text
beta is odd and nonzero
therefore some positive index n has beta_n > 0.
```

Oddness gives only

\[
\beta_{-n}=-\beta_n,
\qquad \beta_0=0.
\]

It does not choose the sign on the positive half-axis.

### Exact falsifier

Take the three ordered nodes

\[
n=(-1,0,1)
\]

and the odd nonzero field

\[
\beta_n=-n=(1,0,-1).
\]

Then, for every `i != j`,

\[
K_{ij}
=\frac{-n_i+n_j}{n_i-n_j}
=-1.
\]

The identity switch already has every off-diagonal entry negative.  Thus:

\[
\boxed{
\text{odd beta + beta not identically zero}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{sign frustration}.
}
\]

For the central source triangle `(-n,0,n)`, when `beta_n != 0`, its cycle sign
is exactly `sign(beta_n)`.  A parity-only FATAL would therefore require the
additional source theorem

\[
\exists n>0,\quad \beta_n>0,
\]

or some other explicitly certified positive triangle.  No such theorem is
supplied by the report or the current source shelf.

The actual selected CCM beta may still be frustrated.  What is killed is the
claimed three-line parity proof, not the mathematical possibility of a later
source-specific frustration certificate.

## 4. Why zero density does not pay the spectral shortcut

The coordinate relation

\[
t_n=\frac{2\pi n}{\log m}
\]

is source-faithful.  It gives a useful scale dictionary between mode index and
physical height.

But a counting law for zeros does not determine a weighted signed functional.
For a fixed number `R` of terms, complex weights of modulus one can add to any
scale between near zero and `R`.  The counting function remembers `R`; it does
not remember the phases or the exact test kernel.

A legal spectral-side theorem would first need an exact completed explicit
formula for each literal Haar coefficient, including all archimedean, prime,
pole, truncation and boundary terms.  It would then need an absolute or signed
kernel bound strong enough to yield the consumer rate

\[
o((\log m)^{-1/2})
\]

on the real axis and the stronger compact rate.  The report provides neither.

Therefore the inference

```text
Haar scale corresponds to a zero-height window
+ zero density is known
=> required Haar energy is small
```

is rejected by C04 and C10.  It is a candidate literature/search direction,
not a theorem input.
