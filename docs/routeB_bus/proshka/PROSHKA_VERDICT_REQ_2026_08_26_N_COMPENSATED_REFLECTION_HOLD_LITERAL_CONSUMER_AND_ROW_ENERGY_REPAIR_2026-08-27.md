## 1. What survives

The report correctly accepts all three repairs from the parent verdict.

First, the W02 tail must be folded because the test is `2*pi`-periodic.  With
`a=L/(4*pi)`, the geometric series gives the exact folded density

\[
\frac{L}{2\pi^2}(\sqrt m-1)e^{-at}\,dt,
\qquad 0<t\le2\pi.
\]

The reflected continuous prime-main density is

\[
\frac{L\sqrt m}{2\pi^2}e^{-at}\,dt,
\]

so their exact difference is

\[
-\frac{L}{2\pi^2}e^{-at}\,dt.
\]

This is genuine completed-source cancellation at leading order.  It is not yet a
bound for the literal von-Mangoldt Stieltjes discrepancy.

Second, after the angle change the archimedean density has the local expansion

\[
d\mu_{\rm arch}(t)\sim\frac{dt}{2\pi t}.
\]

The residue is independent of the schedule.  Reflection creates the opposite
endpoint singularity.  Since the exact off-diagonal test vanishes to first order
at both endpoints, the pairing is locally absolutely integrable.

Third, the report correctly withdraws the statement that the graph-test vector
is normalized.  The vector

These are real representation gains.

## 2. First object repair: full source energy is not finite row energy

The report says that the coefficient used by `physicalFourierEnergy` is the same
formula as `c_n`, hence the two energies are identical.  The displayed formulas
look the same, but their second arguments are different objects.

The physical-energy contract uses

\[
g_k=\operatorname{gTrial}_m(i_k,h_k),
\]

the full unprojected window object.  The finite CCM graph row is the coefficient
row of

\[
k_k=\|P_{N_k}g_k\|^{-1}P_{N_k}g_k.
\]

Writing

\[
s_k=\|P_{N_k}g_k\|^{-1},
\]

we have exactly, for included modes,

\[
q_{k,n}=s_k\langle V_n,g_k\rangle.
\]

Therefore

\[
\|\mathsf Nq_k\|_2^2
=s_k^2\sum_{|n|\le N_k}n^2|\langle V_n,g_k\rangle|^2,
\]

whereas

\[
E_k^{\rm phys}
=\frac{4\pi^2}{L_k^2}
\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}n^2|\langle V_n,g_k\rangle|^2.
\]

The valid source-faithful result is

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathsf Nq_k\|_2^2
\le
s_k^2\frac{L_k^2}{4\pi^2}E_k^{\rm phys}.
}
\]

Equality holds only after replacing full energy by the truncated included-mode
energy and retaining the normalizer.

A one-line plant kills the reported equality: take a full vector with all its
weighted Fourier energy in an omitted mode.  The full physical energy is
positive, while the finite row has zero mode energy.

This is **C04**: same coordinate formula, two different objects.  It is also
**C10**: a full-source energy cannot be substituted for the consumer's finite
normalized-row energy without an adapter.

The damage is repairable.  The repository already contains
`SelectedTrialNormalizerBounded` and a selected-Ferrers theorem that discharges
it under the existing W5/F72 rate ledger.  Hence
`SelectedPhysicalFourierEnergyControl` plus the same normalizer hypotheses would
give

\[
\|\mathsf Nq_k\|_2=O(L_k).
\]

But `SelectedPhysicalFourierEnergyControl` itself remains an open proposition.
No unconditional trial-mode-energy rate has been obtained here.

## 3. Second object repair: reflection sees the off-diagonal channel

The exact completed source consumer is

\[
\Psi_k(z)
=
D_k(z)+I_k(z),
\]

with

\[
D_k(z)=
\sum_i((M_k)_{ii}-a_k)\overline{x_{k,i}(z)}q_{k,i}
\]

and

\[
I_k(z)=\int G_{k,z}(t)\,d\mu_k(t).
\]

The test satisfies

\[
G_{k,z}(2\pi-t)=-G_{k,z}(t).
\]

If

\[
\nu_k=\mu_k-R_*\mu_k,
\qquad
\Phi_k(G)=\langle\nu_k,G\rangle,
\]

then

\[
\Phi_k(G)=2I_k.
\]

Thus the literal identity is

\[
\boxed{
\Psi_k(z)=D_k(z)+\frac12\Phi_k(G_{k,z}).
}
\]

The report's rate assembly omitted both the factor `1/2` and `D_k`.

The previous Volterra/Duhamel identity does not erase this problem.  The full
Volterra kernel contains the linear coefficient

\[
\delta_i=2\overline{x_i}q_i,
\]

which is the diagonal presentation-conversion term.  But that full kernel is not
reflection-odd.  For the one-coordinate plant `n=0`, `x=q=1`, the full kernel is

\[
K(w)=2w,
\]

and

\[
K(1-w)\ne-K(w).
\]

Therefore one cannot simultaneously:

```text
absorb the diagonal into the full Volterra kernel;
and
use the reflection antisymmetry of G.
```

An additional exact shadow decomposition is required.  This is another
**C04/C10** failure.  Representation `R2` applies **C13**: restore the broken
reflection law by carrying the explicit even/diagonal shadow instead of
estimating it away.

A matrix plant says the same thing without formulas: two Hermitian source
matrices may share the same off-diagonal divided-difference data and therefore
the same `G`, while having different diagonals and different literal consumers.
Thus `G` alone never identifies the full source action.
