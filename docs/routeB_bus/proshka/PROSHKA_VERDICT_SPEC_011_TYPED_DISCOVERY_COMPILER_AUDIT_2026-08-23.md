
### 2. What is accepted unchanged

The following parts of SPEC-011 are correct.

1. The semantic model is closer to dependent type theory, a contextual/category-
   with-families view, and a multicategory than to an ordinary category.
2. The operational proof-space is a directed AND-hypergraph. A theorem requiring
   `A ∧ B ∧ C` is one multi-input edge, not three one-input edges.

4. Nonmatching ports may compose only through an explicit theorem-adapter.

6. Forward expansion starts only from semantically admitted suppliers.
7. Backward expansion starts from the exact consumer contract.

### 4. A typed port must carry one shared dependent context

This is the largest missing field in the proposed specification.

Lean hypotheses are not independent plugs. A consumer can require:

```text
A(m,N) ∧ B(m,N) ∧ C(m,N) -> D(m,N).
```

Supplying `A(m1,N1)`, `B(m2,N2)`, and `C(m3,N3)` is invalid even if the three
surface proposition shapes look identical.

Every hyperedge must therefore use one substitution/unification environment for
all of its ports.

Required addition:

```yaml
CONTEXT:
  exact_binders:
  implicit_binders:
  typeclass_environment:
  shared_parameters:
  source_family_key:
  cofinal_schedule_key:
  unifier_substitution:
```

Composition succeeds only if all required ports are discharged under one
coherent substitution. This is the machine version of the current Q3 law:

```text
same object;
same normalization;
same family;
same cofinal path.
```

Without this field the assembler can build a formally plausible Frankenstein
from different cells or different families.

`[ABSTRACT][PAPER]` **[C04]**

### 5. Semantic refinements cannot be naked strings

A plain entry such as

```yaml
normalization: "||f||_L2 = 1"
```

creates a second untrusted kernel in metadata.

Each refinement must carry evidence:

```yaml
REFINEMENT_FACT_V1:
  value:
  evidence_kind: DEFINITIONAL | LEAN_THEOREM | ARB_CERTIFICATE | PAPER | DECLARED
  evidence_ref:
  source_commit_or_bibliographic_pin:
  verifier: LEAN | ARB_INTERVAL | PAPER | CONDITIONAL
  scope: ABSTRACT | FINITE_CELL | COFINAL_FAMILY
  status: PROVED | CONDITIONAL | UNVERIFIED
```

Only `PROVED` or explicitly accepted `CONDITIONAL` facts may constrain active
composition. `DECLARED/UNVERIFIED` metadata is useful for candidate generation,
but it cannot create a proof edge.

This preserves the rule:

```text
Lean type is the kernel-level truth;
metadata may restrict composability;
metadata may never rewrite or strengthen the theorem type.
```

### 8. `LOSS_LEDGER` needs typed composition laws

The proposed ledger mixes a norm factor and an asymptotic cost:

```text
constant_factor = sqrt(2 log K)
budget_cost = O(log K)
```

These are not the same quantity. The norm loss is `sqrt(2 log K)`; a squared
energy estimate may incur `2 log K`.

Use:

```yaml
LOSS_LEDGER_V1:
  exactness: EXACT | ONE_SIDED_BOUND | ASYMPTOTIC
  norm_factor:
  energy_factor:
  additive_error:
  domain_restriction:
  topology_change:
  representative_change:
  quantifier_change:
  rate_map:
  units_map:
  evidence_ref:
```

Minimum composition rules:

```text
multiplicative factors multiply;
additive certified errors add;
domains intersect;
trust takes the weaker verifier;
topology may weaken only through an explicit theorem;
quantifiers and scope never strengthen implicitly;
rate factors are carried to the terminal consumer.
```

A syntactically legal Hoelder adapter may still be unusable if its growing
factor destroys the required cofinal limit. Therefore `preserves quantifier
order` is not enough; the adapter must expose its rate transform.

### 9. `Gap Vector` is a dashboard, not a vector space

The tuple


is valuable, but it has no common addition or scalar multiplication. Keep the
canonical name:

```text

```

or

```text

```

Hard mismatches are terminal for the candidate path. Soft deficits are ranked
only after feasibility.

The lexicographic order is accepted with these guards:

```text
1. all hard gates pass;
2. fewer missing ports/current k;
3. lower residual arity;
4. shorter verified adapter depth;
5. greater catalog CLOSES-minus-OPENS yield;
6. lower total cost;
7. higher kill-power/information gain.
```

`CLOSES` and `OPENS` must be catalog IDs, not newly invented prose labels.

### 10. Backward search does not invert theorems

`Backward unfolding` means reading the exact premises of a consumer and
recursively asking for their suppliers. It does not mean reversing a theorem.

The search must be bounded and honest:

```text
initial depth d = 3;
at most three candidate routes per gap;
visited normalized route fingerprints;
all inputs of an AND-edge must be ready under one context;
explicit Lean harness for every proposed strong edge.
```

Failure to find a path means only:

```text
NO_ROUTE_FOUND_WITHIN_CURRENT_BUDGET.
```

It does not prove that the missing theorem is impossible. `FATAL` requires a
counterexample, an upper-envelope kill, or another proof of impossibility.

### 11. The state machine needs a verifier-scope note

The proposed state sequence is correct for a Lean-produced theorem packet:

```text
RAW_INSIGHT -> GAP_LOCKED -> SOURCE_LOCKED -> CROSSWALK_TYPED
-> FALSIFIER_PASSED -> THEOREM_PACKET_READY -> SOURCE_WRITTEN
-> KERNEL_GREEN -> SEMANTICALLY_ADMITTED -> SHELF_REGISTERED.
```

But `KERNEL_GREEN` means only:

```text
the exact proof term was accepted under the recorded axiom profile.
```

It does not mean that all axioms are mathematically acceptable, and it does not
apply to a paper-only supplier. Paper and interval modules retain their existing
verifier statuses and can enter the active shelf only at the trust floor accepted
by the exact consumer.

Add the holding states already used by the architecture:

