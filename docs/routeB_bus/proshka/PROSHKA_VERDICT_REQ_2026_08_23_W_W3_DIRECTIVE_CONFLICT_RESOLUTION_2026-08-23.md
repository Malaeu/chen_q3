## SEMANTIC AUDIT OF THE PREFLIGHT

### 1. The decisive identity is correctly normalized

For the finite periodization

\[
p_{k,u}(x)=\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}f_k(u(m+x)),
\]

compact support makes the sum finite locally. With the project Fourier convention,

\[
\widehat p_{k,u}(n)=u^{-1}\widehat f_k(n/u).
\]

The packet need not be real-valued. Its exact complex source scale may be retained. What is required is evenness:

\[
f_k(-x)=f_k(x)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\widehat f_k(-\xi)=\widehat f_k(\xi).
\]

Hence, for the period-one Poisson kernel with Fourier coefficients `r^{|n|}`,

\[
\frac{\sqrt u}{2}(P_r*p_{k,u})(0)
=
\frac12u^{-1/2}\widehat f_k(0)
+
u^{-1/2}\sum_{n\ge1}r^n\widehat f_k(n/u),
\]

where the displayed `ν` is `u`; no new symbol or scale is intended. Zero mass removes the first term and gives the exact reflected Abel family. `[ABSTRACT][PAPER]`

The typo-like `ν` in the display above is not part of the contract; the operative formula is

\[
E^{\vee}_{r,k}(u)=u^{-1/2}\sum_{n\ge1}r^n\widehat f_k(n/u).
\]

No empirical scalar is permitted.

### 2. Endpoint seams are not silently repaired

At `u=λ_k/n`, a production endpoint enters the periodization. The positive Poisson kernel converges to the midpoint of the two one-sided values, not necessarily the stored full endpoint value. Therefore the pointwise target is asserted only off the finite seam set. The seam set is `dStar`-null on the selected window, so the midpoint/full-endpoint difference disappears only after passing to the almost-everywhere or `L²` category. `[ABSTRACT][PAPER]` **[C04]**

### 3. The continuous-source Mathlib theorem is the wrong adapter

`Real.fourierCoeff_tsum_comp_add` is reported with a continuous source type. Applying it to the discontinuous production packet would be a source-class substitution. The local proof must instead exploit compact support and finite periodization. `[ABSTRACT][PAPER]` **[C10]**

### 4. Domination is fixed-k, not cofinal

For fixed `k`, `u` lies in one finite multiplicative window. The number of translates that can meet the packet support is uniformly finite over that window. A global packet bound therefore controls the periodization; positivity and unit mass of the Poisson kernel preserve the bound. This is enough for fixed-k dominated convergence in `L²`. It yields no uniform-in-`k` rate. `[COFINAL_FAMILY][PAPER]`
