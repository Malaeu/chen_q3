### 2. The classical Poisson algebra is correct

Fix the Fourier convention



The coefficients and powers follow by separating the zero modes in the full

### 3. Strongest attack: `E_sym` is not the production `E_star`

The project defines

```lean

```

For complex series, the project `tsum` requires the ordinary norm-summable /
unconditionally summable object. A compactly supported function with a nonzero
endpoint jump has Fourier transform of order `1/|xi|`; its samples are therefore
not generally norm-summable. Dirichlet-Jordan supplies a conditionally convergent
**symmetric** sum, not the existing `tsum`.

The elementary plant is the alternating harmonic series: a canonical
conditional/symmetric value exists, while norm summability fails. Therefore

```text

!=
same summation functional.
```

This is an exact **C04** and **C10** kill. The report may retain the classical
Poisson identity, but it may not write its reflected term as the production
`E_star(hTrialHat)` until a new canonical summation object and a crosswalk are
proved.

The smallest repair is Abel regularization. For `0 < r < 1`, define

```text

```

The series is absolutely convergent for every `r<1`. The next preflight must
decide whether the limit `r -> 1-` exists in L2 on the exact selected window,
agrees with the Dirichlet-Jordan symmetric sum, and belongs to the shifted form
domain with a usable bound.
