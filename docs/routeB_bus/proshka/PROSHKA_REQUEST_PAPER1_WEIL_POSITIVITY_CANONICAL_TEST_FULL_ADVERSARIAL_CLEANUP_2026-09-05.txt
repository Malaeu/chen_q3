     Q(f_0 s) = ∫ b E_s + Σ w_n E_s(log n) with the closed form b(t) = a(t) − 2cosh(t/2) = e^{-5t/2}/(1−e^{-2t}) − e^{t/2} and the sign change

     Riemann in CCM's normalisation (2511.22755 Lemma 7.1); the log-variable form to Freedman 2606.29555 eq. (1).

     Suzuki's positive definiteness on C_c^∞ under RH and Bombieri's Lemma 10 (non-degenerate truncations).
