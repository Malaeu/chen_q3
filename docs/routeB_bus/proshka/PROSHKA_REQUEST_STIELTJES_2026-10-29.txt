N1  Closed form of your residue. From (4.8) at a simple odd bottom:

    hence sign R_m = (-1)^(number of even eigenvalues below mu).

    Consequence: the residue test is a counting statement ("an odd number of even
    levels below the odd bottom"), not a cheaper object. G1a is "at least one".

N2  The residue sign hides in a cancellation. v0.y = (A0 - mu)(d.y) - (B^-2 beta_o).y.
    The two terms agree to 6 or more digits from m = 6 (m = 6: 0.026021 and

N3  f_m is the reciprocal of one resolvent element (observer, by Cramer from (4.9)):
      f_m(t) = 1 / g_m(t),
      g_m(t) = <eta, (K_m - tI)^-1 e_0> = h^T (E - tI)^-1 e_0 = det(O - tI) / det(E - tI).
    Spectral form over the even eigenpairs (lambda_i, xi_i), full coordinates:
      g_m(t) = sum_i w_i / (lambda_i - t),   w_i = (eta.xi_i) * xi_i(0),   sum_i w_i = eta.e_0 = 1.
    Checked: |f g - 1| <= 3e-96 at dps 120, and sum w_i = 1, on m = 2..16.
    So g_m is the Stieltjes transform of a signed measure with total mass 1.
    Its poles are the even eigenvalues; its zeros are the odd eigenvalues.

N4  Consequences (observer argument; please check):
    (a) G1a <=> w_0 > 0 and g_m has no zero on (-inf, lambda_0(E)).
        Reason: g_m -> 0+ at -inf (mass 1). A zero below lambda_0(E) is an odd
        eigenvalue below the even bottom. If w_0 = 0, lambda_0(E) is also an odd
        eigenvalue. If w_0 < 0, g_m -> -inf at lambda_0(E)- and crosses zero below it.
    (b) Necessary for G1a: w_0 = (eta.xi_0) xi_0(0) > 0. The ground endpoint value

    (c) Sufficient for G1a (Abel summation, signs only, no difference):
