## Normalized one-point innovation explains the full margin — rejected

HYPOTHESIS: the old scalar scale sigma²/(2Psi(Delta)) near .2 describes
the full fresh section's clearance from the wall. This exploratory question
is distinguished from the preregistered arithmetic-section bound.

EVIDENCE: at a=6,Delta=1/32 the endpoint margin is .192005, but adding
fresh nodes reduces it to .0870618 at q128. q32-to128 changes only .0004281.
Independent MP80 gives q32=.08748990968134. The generic one-point identity
is correct, but the proposed identification with the full margin is not.

WHAT KILLS IT: this supported discrepancy already does. Do not use the
one-point innovation as a numerical value or lower bound for mu_full.

HANDOFF: preserve the complete F inverse and all response columns. Positive
finite mu_q is an upper approximant; its sign cannot certify the continuum.

## Fixed-history small-step clearance — finite normalization supported

HYPOTHESIS: for one fixed positive old history, sufficiently small delta
drives clearance toward1 uniformly over finite fresh-node choices. Review
preregistration and source local bound specify its conditional scope.

EVIDENCE: four-anchor section16 q32 margins at delta=.0606246,.001,.00001
are .450005,.973570,.999533; dyadic refinement decreases each family.
The source's beta/rho bound has rho/beta tending to0 for fixed history.

WHAT KILLS IT: a guarded negative finite Schur extension at arbitrarily
small lengths for this same fixed history, or a source normalization failure.
The planted world also has the local property; this is not a discriminator.

HANDOFF: the simultaneous growth/refinement of the old history needs a
separate budget. A local theorem cannot be iterated with an unpaid length.

## Constant margin and maximum admissible length — not interchangeable

HYPOTHESIS: a decreasing positive mu implies a finite collapsing delta-star.

EVIDENCE: at fine a=6 the sampled margin falls to .0000966932 at delta=.25
without any detected failure. Under RH every finite extension is positive,
so the unrestricted all-node step supremum is infinite. A fixed node-count
grid moving with delta is not nested and cannot locate that supremum.

WHAT KILLS IT: this implication is not warranted by the measurements.
The bounded search stops at1/4; no extrapolation or tuned root is fitted.

HANDOFF: use nested supports and keep a failed length, an unobserved failure,
and a small but positive normalized clearance as different outcomes.

## Planted boundary comparison — finite diagnostic supported

HYPOTHESIS: the old rank-four planted hazard margin 1-lambda_max(Theta)
and a new extension margin
identify the same first failed combined node set, with their required
positive old/fresh guards. Uniform preregistration gives probability .99.

EVIDENCE: the fine old/future endpoint comparison reproduces the previous
1.03125 first failure. Numerical margins differ; the earlier 2x2 hazard
normalizes against a different positive baseline.

WHAT KILLS IT: a sign mismatch on identical nodes after independent source
and guard checks. Extra fresh nodes or a different old history are not
identical tests and may detect failure earlier.

HANDOFF: signed Schur congruence explains the boundary agreement. It is not
a new source inequality controlling the zeta sign. See sections/RESULT.md
and component cards for the arithmetic-section Mythos test.

## Arithmetic-section floor .1 — rejected numerically

HYPOTHESIS: min mu(t,log((n+1)/n)), n<=240, remains at least .1.
Mythos prior: .4, retained in sections/PREREGISTRATION.md.

EVIDENCE: minimum one-point margins over n2..240 are .179551,.140119,
.111055,.0915735 for old meshes1/32,1/64,1/128,1/256. Adding fresh nodes
at fixed section16 gives mu_32=.0616361,.0251066,.00951924,.00377376.
Where the full fresh form is positive, the exact infinite-future margin
is no larger than these finite values.
The section lies inside the source's short coercivity range.

Independent MP80 checks give .0915734519013928067 for mesh256,n30,q1
with all871old directions, and .0616360758945333 for mesh32,n16,q32.
Differences from the float scan are at most9.95e-15 and4.61e-14,
respectively. The n16 bound already fails at q4=.0833588792525536.

WHAT KILLS IT: the validated finite upper approximant below .1 already
rejects the proposed full-future floor. Do not tune the floor or exclude
the section. Positive finite values do not establish mu_full>=0.

HANDOFF: distinguish a quantified uniform margin from positivity itself.
Old-history refinement enlarges the predictor space, so its monotone loss
of clearance is generic. The present four resolutions do not establish a
power law, vanishing limiting margin, or collapsed true extension radius.
