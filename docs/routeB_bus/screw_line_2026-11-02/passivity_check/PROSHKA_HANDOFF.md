Lambda(n)/sqrt(n) terms. For a section [log n,log(n+1)], retain every old
interaction. Suppose an arbitrary finite collection of old nodes gives a
strictly positive G_old. For any new nodes in that section, prove

    G_new,new - G_new,old G_old^-1 G_old,new >= 0.          (P)

The proof must derive (P) from the original source; it may not assume
global G>=0, RH, or a positive Hamiltonian reconstructed from that desired
