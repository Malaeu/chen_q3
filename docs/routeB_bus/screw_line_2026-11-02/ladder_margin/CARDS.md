## Finite arithmetic ladder positivity — certified finite result

HYPOTHESIS: the specified exact finite node alphabets through log3,log5,log7
have positive source Gram matrices, including all admitted prime-power shifts.

EVIDENCE: six distinct matrices,49/107/166nodes at M32 and97/211/329nodes
at M64, have strictly positive directed lower eigenvalue bounds. Direct
interval LDL certifies M32; exact dyadic residual witnesses certify M64.
Root replay of the largest cell returns lambda_min>0.0001172554057190589.

WHAT KILLS IT: a failed verified source enclosure, invalid interval remainder,
unresolved exact threshold, nonpositive residual lower endpoint or witness
hash mismatch. Retained direct-LDL wrapping failures were not accepted as
either a sign certificate or a counterexample.

HANDOFF: these certificates cover the listed finite nodes, principal
submatrices and semidefinite repetitions. They do not extend the cardinality
or erase unobserved points. Actual between-node work needs a different norm
budget, such as a complete source-relative coercive/Schur comparison.
