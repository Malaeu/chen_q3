## Finite knot sets are positive — certified in the stated finite scope

HYPOTHESIS: the complete arithmetic screw source is positive definite on
the specified uniform-plus-forward-shift node sets in log3,log5,log7.
Prior .99 for M32 and .8 for the denser repeat; node definitions and
acceptance criteria were recorded before execution.

EVIDENCE: all six matrices certify, with dimensions49,107,166 atM32 and
97,211,329 atM64. Direct outward LDL handles M32 and W3/M64. Validated
exact-dyadic residual certificates handle all M64 cases. Conservative
lower eigenvalue bounds and exact witnesses are retained. The largest
witness was replayed with NumPy and SciPy disabled. W3/M32 also passes
a768-bit/1e-60 series-tail replay; all49pivot intervals are nested.

WHAT KILLS IT: failure of the supplied directed/exact-integer checker,
an invalid source identity or threshold comparison, or mismatch of fixed
node/source/witness bytes. Approximate eigenvalues do not accept a case.

HANDOFF: these finite listed configurations, their principal subblocks
and repetitions are paid. They do not pay arbitrary between-node points,
arbitrary cardinality, all window lengths, or a global positivity criterion.

## Finite positivity plus a continuity modulus certifies all nodes — not obtained

HYPOTHESIS: nearest-grid interpolation of the positive matrices might
control every configuration on the entire interval. This is a method
candidate, not an assumed theorem.

EVIDENCE: the explicit source modulus gives a matrix perturbation budget
N[omega(2epsilon)+2omega(epsilon)]. At the nearest-grid error the budgets
are about31,83,146, while the eigenvalue lower bounds are around1e-4.
The lower estimate is negative, so this method fails to certify the gap.
Collisions under nearest-grid mapping and unbounded cardinality are
additional structural obstructions. No negative source vector is inferred.

WHAT KILLS THE METHOD: the recorded coarse budgets already do. Do not
turn them into a kernel counterexample or infer a cardinality-uniform
positive floor from a finite minimum. Mere mesh refinement is not the
missing operator estimate.

HANDOFF: an all-node coercive/Schur return controlling the complete source
between the knots is unpaid. The modulus does certify small neighborhoods
of the same fixed configurations, with radii explicitly stored in
INTERSTITIAL_BUDGET.json; that weaker result cannot be promoted to all nodes.

## Direct interval LDL width is a physical sign detector — rejected

HYPOTHESIS: the unresolved direct LDL pivot at M64 might reflect a source
negative direction. No such sign claim was accepted.

EVIDENCE: its enclosure crosses zero at pivot159 in W5 and W7. The same
matrices are then positive under independent exact-dyadic backward-error
verification. The original failed records are preserved. This demonstrates
interval wrapping rather than a source counterexample.

WHAT KILLS IT: the positive validated residual certificate already excludes
a negative direction in the very same finite matrix.

HANDOFF: keep source signs separate from conditioning of a verification
algorithm. All numerical matrix factors remain untrusted until the exact
integer and directed source residual inequalities pass.
