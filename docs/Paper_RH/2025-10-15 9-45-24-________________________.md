# Лемма (локальная конечность прайм-сэмплера)

Пусть \(K>0\). Тогда для любой чётной \(\Phi\in C_c(\mathbb R)\) с \(\operatorname{supp}\Phi\subset[-K,K]\) прайм-часть функционала
\[
\sum_{n\ge 2}\frac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,\Phi(\xi_n),\qquad \xi_n:=\frac{\log n}{2\pi},
\]
имеет **лишь конечное число ненулевых слагаемых**, то есть на каждом компакте прайм-сэмплер есть конечная сумма точечных функционалов.

## Доказательство

1) Узлы прайм-сэмплера заданы дискретным множеством \(\{\xi_n\}_{n\ge2}\) с \(\xi_n=\frac{\log n}{2\pi}\) (см. T0/нормировку и определение \(Q\))【1:0†main_clean.pdf†L13-L21】【2:3†main_clean.pdf†L24-L33】. Тогда условие \(|\xi_n|\le K\) эквивалентно \(n\le N(K):=\lfloor e^{2\pi K}\rfloor\), откуда «активных» узлов на \([-K,K]\) конечное число (FAQ-1)【2:6†Q*.pdf†L9-L17】. В частности, для \(\operatorname{supp}\Phi\subset[-K,K]\) все члены с \(|\xi_n|>K\) обнуляются и прайм-сумма конечна (лемма 10.5)【1:13†main_clean.pdf†L42-L52】.

2) Более того, на каждом компакте имеется **положительный минимальный зазор** между узлами:
\[
\delta_K=\min_{\alpha_m,\alpha_n\in[-K,K],\,m\ne n}|\alpha_m-\alpha_n|\;\ge\;\frac{1}{2\pi(\lfloor e^{2\pi K}\rfloor+1)}\;\ge\;\frac{1}{4\pi e^{2\pi K}}\;>\;0,
\]
см. явные нижние оценки (лемма 12.5/12.13, а также запись в «Parameter Recipe»)【2:4†main_clean.pdf†L1-L5】【2:12†Q*.pdf†L87-L92】【2:9†main_clean.pdf†L5-L11】. Это подчёркивает отсутствие точек накопления узлов внутри \([-K,K]\), но для локальной конечности достаточно п. 1.

Тем самым прайм-часть \(Q\) на фиксированном компакте есть конечная сумма точечных оценок \(\Phi(\xi_n)\) с весами \(2\Lambda(n)/\sqrt n\).

\(\square\)

# Следствие (непрерывность/липшицевость на компакте)

Из леммы следует, что для \(\Phi_1,\Phi_2\in C_c([-K,K])\)
\[
\Big|\sum_{\xi_n\in[-K,K]}\tfrac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,\big(\Phi_1(\xi_n)-\Phi_2(\xi_n)\big)\Big|
\;\le\;\Big(\sum_{\xi_n\in[-K,K]}\tfrac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\Big)\,\|\Phi_1-\Phi_2\|_\infty.
\]
С другой стороны, архимедова часть непрерывна в \(L^1([-K,K])\) и ограничена операторной нормой \(\|a^\ast\|_{L^\infty([-K,K])}\) на компакте, откуда стандартно:
\[
|Q(\Phi_1)-Q(\Phi_2)|\;\le\;L_Q(K)\,\|\Phi_1-\Phi_2\|_\infty,\quad 
L_Q(K):=\|a\|_{L^1([-K,K])}+\sum_{\xi_n\in[-K,K]}\frac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}.
\]
Это — ровно та липшицева оценка, которая уже зафиксирована в A2【1:13†main_clean.pdf†L1-L12】. В частности, если \(\Phi\) получена как Fejér×heat-окно и мы продлили её нулём вне \([-K,K]\), то «хвост» вне компакта гасится гауссовской в логарифме оценкой после конечной отсечки по \(n\) (см. пункт про tail в A2)【1:13†main_clean.pdf†L13-L17】.

```tex
\begin{lemma}[Local finiteness of the prime sampler]
Fix $K>0$. For every even $\Phi\in C_c(\mathbb{R})$ with $\mathrm{supp}\,\Phi\subset[-K,K]$, the prime part of $Q$,
\[
\sum_{n\ge 2}\frac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,\Phi(\xi_n),\qquad \xi_n:=\frac{\log n}{2\pi},
\]
is a \emph{finite} sum (only finitely many terms are nonzero).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
In the T0 normalization, prime nodes are placed at $\xi_n=\log n/(2\pi)$ and the functional is
\[
Q(\Phi)=\int_{\mathbb{R}} a^\ast(\xi)\Phi(\xi)\,d\xi\;-\;\sum_{n\ge 2}\frac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,\Phi(\xi_n),\quad a^\ast(\xi)=2\pi\bigl(\log\pi-\Re\psi(\tfrac14+i\pi\xi)\bigr). 
\]
Hence $\Phi(\xi_n)=0$ whenever $|\xi_n|>K$; but $|\xi_n|\le K$ is equivalent to $n\le \lfloor e^{2\pi K}\rfloor$, so only finitely many nodes fall in $[-K,K]$ and the prime sum is finite. (See the T0/Weil form and node placement【turn3file1†main_clean.pdf†L48-L61】【turn3file5†main_clean.pdf†L24-L31】.) A minimal log–spacing on $[-K,K]$ is explicitly available,
\[
\delta_K:=\min_{m\ne n,\;\alpha_m,\alpha_n\in[-K,K]}|\alpha_m-\alpha_n|\;\ge\;\frac{1}{2\pi(\lfloor e^{2\pi K}\rfloor+1)}\;>\;0,
\]
which records the absence of accumulation points but is not needed for finiteness【turn3file9†main_clean.pdf†L1-L5】.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Lipschitz continuity on a fixed compact]
For $\Phi_1,\Phi_2\in C_c([-K,K])$ one has
\[
|Q(\Phi_1)-Q(\Phi_2)|\le 
\bigl\|a^\ast\bigr\|_{L^\infty([-K,K])}\,2K\,\|\Phi_1-\Phi_2\|_\infty
\;+\;\Bigl(\sum_{\xi_n\in[-K,K]}\frac{2\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\Bigr)\,\|\Phi_1-\Phi_2\|_\infty.
\]
In particular, $Q$ is Lipschitz on $C_c([-K,K])$ with the stated explicit constant.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The Archimedean term is continuous in $\Phi$ in $L^1([-K,K])$ because $a^\ast$ is bounded on the compact; the prime term is a finite sum of point evaluations by the lemma, hence continuous as well. See also Lemma~10.6 for the same argument packaged in the text【turn3file3†main_clean.pdf†L1-L10】.
\end{proof}

### Regularity \(a\in C^1\), boundary conditions, and BV → explicit Lipschitz of \(P_A\)

\begin{lemma}[Symbol Lipschitz from $C^1$/BV input]
Let $a^\ast(\xi)=2\pi(\log\pi-\Re\psi(\tfrac14+i\pi\xi))$ be the Archimedean density from T0 and $P_A$ the Toeplitz symbol induced by an even Fej\'er$\times$heat window $\Phi_{B,t,\tau}$. Then $P_A\in\mathrm{Lip}(1)$ and
\[
\omega_{P_A}(h)\;\le\;L_A\,h,\qquad h\in(0,\pi],
\]
with an explicit $L_A$ depending on $\|a\|_{L^\infty([-B,B])}$, $\|a'\|_{L^\infty([-B,B])}$, and the (bounded-variation) profile of the generator (cf.\ A3 Lemma~11.27 and the A3 summary)【4:main_clean.pdf†L30-L41】.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Write $P_A(\theta)=A_0+2\sum_{k\ge1}A_k\cos(k\theta)$ with $A_k=\int a(\xi)\Phi_{B,t,\tau}(\xi)\cos(k\xi)\,d\xi$. Summing the cosine series with the Dirichlet kernel then yields $|P_A(\theta+h)-P_A(\theta)|\le L_A h$ for $h\in(0,\pi]$. This is the standard BV$\to$Lipschitz estimate used in A3, see Lemma~11.27 in the A3 section summary【4:main_clean.pdf†L30-L41】.
\end{proof}
```

% === RKHS–Weil isometry and Rayleigh identity (trace-only) ===

\begin{definition}[Working set and kernel]
Fix a compact $[-K,K]$ and a base generator $\Phi_{B,t_0,\tau}$ (Fej\'er$\times$heat; even, nonnegative).
Let $V_K := \operatorname{span}\{\Phi_{B,t_0,\tau} : |\tau| + B \le K\}\subset W_K$ be the working subspace on the Weil side.
Define the positive semidefinite kernel on parameter space
\[
\mathbf{K}(\tau,\sigma)\ :=\ Q\!\big(\Phi_{B,t_0,\tau},\,\Phi_{B,t_0,\sigma}\big),
\]
using polarization of $Q$ on even tests.
Let $\mathcal{H}_{\mathbf{K}}$ be the RKHS with reproducing kernel $\mathbf{K}$ and canonical representers $k_\tau(\cdot)=\mathbf{K}(\cdot,\tau)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[RKHS–Weil isometry on $V_K$]\label{lem:rkhs-weil-isometry}
There is a unique linear map
\[
\mathcal{U}\colon \mathcal{H}_{\mathbf{K}}\longrightarrow \overline{V_K}^{\,Q}
\quad\text{with}\quad
\mathcal{U}\,k_\tau\ =\ \Phi_{B,t_0,\tau},
\]
which extends by linearity and completion to a surjective \emph{isometry} between $(\mathcal{H}_{\mathbf{K}},\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{\mathcal{H}_{\mathbf{K}}})$ and the Hilbert space completion of $V_K$ under the inner product induced by $Q$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For any finite families $\{\tau_i\}_{i=1}^m$, $\{\sigma_j\}_{j=1}^n$ and scalars $c_i,d_j$,
\[
\Big\langle \sum_i c_i k_{\tau_i},\ \sum_j d_j k_{\sigma_j}\Big\rangle_{\mathcal{H}_{\mathbf{K}}}
\ =\ \sum_{i,j} c_i \overline{d_j}\,\mathbf{K}(\tau_i,\sigma_j)
\ =\ \sum_{i,j} c_i \overline{d_j}\,Q\!\big(\Phi_{B,t_0,\tau_i},\Phi_{B,t_0,\sigma_j}\big),
\]
by the reproducing property. The right-hand side is precisely the Gram pairing of $\sum_i c_i\Phi_{B,t_0,\tau_i}$ and $\sum_j d_j\Phi_{B,t_0,\sigma_j}$ in the $Q$–inner product, hence $\mathcal{U}$ preserves inner products on the algebraic span and extends by density to an isometry onto $\overline{V_K}^{\,Q}$. Uniqueness follows from $\mathcal{U}k_\tau=\Phi_{B,t_0,\tau}$ and linearity.
\end{proof}

Under the T0 normalization, for any trigonometric $p\in L^2(\mathbb{T})$ (with $\|p\|_2=1$) and any even Fej\'er$\times$heat window $\Phi$ one has
\[

\ =\ \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} P_A(\theta)\,|p(\theta)|^2\,d\theta
\ -\ \sum_{\text{active }n} w(n)\,\Phi(\xi_n)\,|p(\xi_n)|^2,
\]
and at the constant test $p\equiv 1$ the right-hand side equals $Q(\Phi)$; equivalently,
\[
\langle (T_A - T_P)1,1\rangle_{L^2(\mathbb{T})}=Q(\Phi).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
This is the standard Rayleigh identification for Toeplitz+sampling on the trigonometric subspace:
$T_A$ acts by the symbol $P_A$, and $T_P$ samples at the symmetric prime nodes with weights $w(n)$.
The T0 crosswalk matches the resulting quadratic form with $Q(\Phi)$ at $p\equiv 1$.
\end{proof}
